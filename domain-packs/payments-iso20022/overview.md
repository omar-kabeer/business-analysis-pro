# Payments and ISO 20022 Pack

Knowledge and deliverables for business analysis on payments initiatives, centred on the ISO 20022 messaging standard. Load this pack when an initiative involves moving money, payment messaging, clearing and settlement, or payments regulation.

## When to load

Any mention of ISO 20022, SWIFT, MT or MX messages, SEPA, TARGET2 or T2, Fedwire, CHAPS, pain or pacs or camt messages, clearing and settlement, instant payments, or a payments product or scheme.

## What it covers

- `terminology.md`: how ISO 20022 is structured, the main message families, message identifiers, and the usage guidelines (CBPR+, HVPS+).
- `regulation.md`: the standards and regulatory landscape an analyst must account for, with dates and a note to verify current status.
- `deliverables/`: payments-specific templates (message mapping, reconciliation requirements, and a payments compliance checklist).

## How it plugs into the OS

- The business-analysis and requirements skills use the terminology and deliverables here for payments requirements.
- The architecture skill draws on the message and interface detail for integration.
- The governance and quality skills use the compliance checklist as a control.

## A note on accuracy

Payments standards and regulations change on fixed timelines. The dates in `regulation.md` were current as of mid-2026 and are cited. Always verify the current position with the standards body or regulator before relying on it for a decision.
