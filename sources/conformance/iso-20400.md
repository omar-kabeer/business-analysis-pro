# Conformance checks: iso-20400

Source id: `iso-20400`
Standard: ISO 20400, Sustainable procurement, Guidance.
Edition held: First edition, 2017-04 (ISO 20400:2017(E)).
Publisher: ISO.
Authority: normative.
Identity: confirmed from the title page on 2026-08-07. First edition 2017.
Licence: ISO copyright, restricted; paraphrase only.
Modality note: ISO 20400 is a guidance standard. It states its provisions with "should" and is not certifiable. The blocking severities below are our house gate that a procurement artefact must evidence the named element; the standard states each as recommended practice. This is recorded so no reader mistakes a house gate for an ISO mandate.
Artefact types constrained: `procurement-document`, `vendor-assessment`.
Owning skills: `procurement-contracts` (procurement-document), `vendor-evaluation` (vendor-assessment).
Locator style: ISO 20400 clause `<n>`.

## How to read this file

Three layers, kept separate: Source (S) with locator, labelled Inference (I), and the Check (C). Severity here is a house-gate decision, not a reading of the standard's verbs.

## Checks: procurement-document

Applies to a procurement specification, tender, or sourcing document.

| Check id | Question (yes = pass) | Severity | Locator | How the reviewer confirms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ISO20400-PD-01 | Does the document define and document sustainability criteria for the goods or services being procured? | blocking | 7.3, 7.3.1 | Confirm sustainability criteria are present. A procurement document with none fails. |
| ISO20400-PD-02 | Do the sustainability criteria reflect the priorities set in the sourcing strategy (for example the key requirements appear as minimum criteria)? | blocking | 7.3.1 | Confirm the criteria trace to the defined sourcing priorities. Criteria unconnected to any priority fail. |
| ISO20400-PD-03 | Is each sustainability requirement verifiable through an evaluation procedure outlined in the tender documents? | blocking | 7.3.5 | Confirm each requirement names how it will be evaluated. A requirement with no evaluation method fails. |
| ISO20400-PD-04 | Are minimum (mandatory) and optional sustainability requirements distinguished? | advisory | 7.3.3 | Look for the minimum-versus-optional split. Advisory. |
| ISO20400-PD-05 | Do the criteria and any go or no-go gates give all suppliers a full and fair opportunity to compete? | advisory | 7.2.6, 7.3 | Judge fairness of the criteria and gates. Advisory. |

## Checks: vendor-assessment

Applies to a supplier assessment or selection artefact.

| Check id | Question (yes = pass) | Severity | Locator | How the reviewer confirms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ISO20400-VA-01 | Does the assessment evaluate suppliers against the defined sustainability criteria and requirements? | blocking | 7.4, 7.3.5 | Confirm the assessment scores suppliers on the sustainability criteria. An assessment ignoring them fails. |
| ISO20400-VA-02 | Does the assessment consider each supplier's capacity to meet the sustainability requirements, including through the supply chain? | blocking | 7.4.1, 4.5.1 | Confirm supplier capacity to meet sustainability requirements is assessed. Absence fails. |
| ISO20400-VA-03 | Is the evaluation procedure chosen with regard to the risk of non-conformity with the sustainability criteria? | advisory | 7.3.5 | Check the evaluation method reflects non-conformity risk. Advisory. |
| ISO20400-VA-04 | Are prequalification and tender criteria applied consistently and fairly across suppliers? | advisory | 7.4.2, 7.4.3 | Judge consistency of application. Advisory. |

### Provenance, procurement-document

- PD-01. S: Once the sourcing strategy is decided, the sustainability criteria should be defined and documented, in the specification or in related documents (7.3, 7.3.1). I: Gated as a house requirement that the document carry sustainability criteria.
- PD-02. S: The sustainability criteria should reflect the priorities defined in the sourcing strategy, for example by including key sustainability requirements in the minimum sustainability criteria (7.3.1). I: none.
- PD-03. S: Each sustainability requirement should be verifiable via an evaluation procedure that is outlined by the organization in the tender documents (7.3.5). I: none.
- PD-04. S: The organization applies minimum and optional requirements (7.3.3). I: Advisory distinction.
- PD-05. S: Care should be taken to ensure all suppliers are given full and fair opportunity to compete, including at any go or no-go criteria (7.2.6, 7.3). I: Advisory fairness judgement.

### Provenance, vendor-assessment

- VA-01. S: Selecting suppliers includes assessing them against the sustainability criteria, which should be verifiable via the evaluation procedure (7.4, 7.3.5). I: Gated as a house requirement that the assessment uses the criteria.
- VA-02. S: Risk management considers how suppliers throughout the supply chains are capable of meeting sustainability requirements; selecting suppliers includes assessing the capacity of suppliers (4.5.1, 7.4.1). I: none.
- VA-03. S: When choosing an evaluation procedure, organizations should take into account the risks of non-conformity with the sustainability criteria (7.3.5). I: Advisory.
- VA-04. S: Prequalifying suppliers and managing tenders should give fair opportunity (7.4.2, 7.4.3). I: Advisory consistency check.

## Not-testable obligations

| Obligation (paraphrase) | Locator | Why not testable on a finished artefact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The principles and core subjects of sustainable procurement (accountability, transparency, human rights, ethical behaviour, and the rest). | 4.2, 4.3 | Value statements about the procurement effort, not properties of a single procurement document or supplier assessment. |
| Organizing the procurement function: governance, culture, performance management, stakeholder engagement. | 5, 6 | Constrain the organization and its procurement function, not the artefact under review. |
| Managing and reviewing the contract, supplier relationship, and end of life. | 7.5, 7.6 | Post-award activities over the contract life, not the procurement document or the pre-award assessment. |

## Coverage

- `procurement-document`: 3 blocking checks (PD-01 to PD-03), 2 advisory (PD-04, PD-05).
- `vendor-assessment`: 2 blocking checks (VA-01, VA-02), 2 advisory (VA-03, VA-04).

Both constrained artefact types carry a blocking check (forward cross-check satisfied). Every check maps to a declared artefact type, no orphans. No other normative source constrains these two types, so no conflict.
