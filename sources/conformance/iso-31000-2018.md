# Conformance checks: iso-31000-2018

Source id: `iso-31000-2018`
Standard: ISO 31000, Risk management, Guidelines.
Edition held: Second edition, 2018-02 (ISO 31000:2018(E)).
Publisher: ISO.
Authority: normative.
Identity: confirmed from the title page on 2026-08-07. Second edition 2018.
Licence caveat: the copy held carries a Clarivate Techstreet watermark licensing it to a University of Toronto user, dated 2018-03-11, with no reproduction or distribution permitted. It is not licensed to us. Identity is confirmed but the copy is not ours. Replace with an owned copy before this underpins a client deliverable. The checks below paraphrase obligations and reproduce no clause text at length.
Modality caveat: ISO 31000 is a guidelines standard. It states its provisions with "should", not "shall", and it is explicitly not intended for certification. It therefore mandates nothing in the conformance sense. The severities below are our house gate, not the standard's. A blocking check means our OS requires the artefact to evidence that element before delivery; the standard states the same element as recommended practice. This is recorded so no reader mistakes a house gate for an ISO mandate.
Artefact types constrained: `risk-assessment`, `risk-register`.
Owning skills: `risk-analysis` (primary), `governance`.
Locator style: ISO 31000:2018 clause `<n>`.

## How to read this file

Three layers, kept separate.

- Source (S). What the standard says, paraphrased, with the clause locator. ISO 31000 modality is "should" unless noted.
- Inference (I). Our reasoning connecting the clause to our artefacts, including any decision to gate on a "should".
- Check (C). The reviewer instruction, a yes-or-no question answerable from the finished artefact without opening the standard.

Severity here is a house-gate decision (see the modality caveat), not a reading of the standard's verbs.

## Checks: risk-assessment

Applies to a risk assessment artefact (the identification, analysis, and evaluation of risks for a defined scope).

| Check id | Question (yes = pass) | Severity | Locator | How the reviewer confirms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ISO31000-RA-01 | Does the assessment contain all three elements: identified risks, an analysis of each, and an evaluation of each against criteria? | blocking | 6.4.1 | Confirm the artefact shows the three stages. An artefact that lists risks but never analyses or evaluates them fails. |
| ISO31000-RA-02 | Are the scope and the external and internal context of the assessment stated? | blocking | 6.3.2, 6.3.3 | Look for a stated scope (level, objectives, inclusions and exclusions) and a context statement. An assessment with no stated scope is uninterpretable and fails. |
| ISO31000-RA-03 | Are risk criteria defined, stating how significance is judged (how consequence and likelihood are measured and how the level of risk is determined)? | blocking | 6.3.4 | Find the criteria the evaluation uses. Missing criteria fail, because evaluation then has nothing to compare against. |
| ISO31000-RA-04 | Is each risk analysed for both likelihood and consequence, yielding a level of risk? | blocking | 6.4.3 | For each risk confirm a likelihood and a consequence (or an explicit combined level). A bare risk name with no analysis fails. |
| ISO31000-RA-05 | Does each risk carry an evaluation outcome, comparing its analysis against the criteria to a decision (accept, treat, analyse further, or reconsider)? | blocking | 6.4.4 | Confirm each analysed risk resolves to a decision. An analysis with no evaluation decision fails. |
| ISO31000-RA-06 | Is each risk described with its sources or causes, its events, and its consequences on objectives? | advisory | 6.4.2 | The standard lists these as factors to consider. Flag risks stated as one-liners with no cause or consequence. |
| ISO31000-RA-07 | Are the assumptions, limitations, and divergent views behind the analysis documented and communicated to decision makers? | advisory | 6.4.3 | Look for a note of assumptions, data limitations, and confidence. Absence is a quality flag, not a fail. |
| ISO31000-RA-08 | Is the evaluation outcome recorded and validated at an appropriate level of the organization? | advisory | 6.4.4 | Confirm sign-off or validation of the evaluation. Advisory: this is an organizational act the artefact may only reference. |

## Checks: risk-register

Applies to a risk register (the recorded, maintained record of risks, their treatment, and ownership).

| Check id | Question (yes = pass) | Severity | Locator | How the reviewer confirms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ISO31000-RR-01 | Does each register entry record the risk and its analysis (likelihood, consequence, level)? | blocking | 6.7, 6.4.3 | Confirm each row carries the analysis, not just a risk title. A title-only register fails. |
| ISO31000-RR-02 | Does each entry record a treatment decision (avoid, remove source, change likelihood or consequence, share, or retain) or a documented decision to accept the remaining risk? | blocking | 6.5.1, 6.5.2 | Confirm each risk has a treatment option or an explicit informed acceptance. An untreated, unaccepted risk fails. |
| ISO31000-RR-03 | Is an accountable or responsible owner recorded for each risk or its treatment? | blocking | 6.5.3 | Confirm a named owner or role per entry. Unowned risks fail. |
| ISO31000-RR-04 | Is the remaining (residual) risk after treatment documented and flagged for monitoring? | advisory | 6.5.2 | Look for residual risk and a monitoring note. Advisory where treatment is not yet implemented. |
| ISO31000-RR-05 | Where a treatment plan is referenced, does it carry rationale, actions, resources, owners, performance measures, constraints, reporting, and timing? | advisory | 6.5.3 | Check the referenced plan against this content list. Advisory: the plan may be a separate artefact. |
| ISO31000-RR-06 | Are entries dated and kept under review, showing the register is maintained rather than a one-off snapshot? | advisory | 6.6 | Look for entry dates, review dates, or a change history. Absence flags a stale register. |

### Provenance, risk-assessment

- RA-01. S: Risk assessment is the overall process of risk identification, risk analysis and risk evaluation (6.4.1). I: We gate on the artefact evidencing all three, since an artefact missing a stage is not a risk assessment in the standard's terms.
- RA-02. S: The organization should define the scope of its risk management activities (6.3.2); the context should be established from the external and internal environment (6.3.3). I: An assessment with no scope or context cannot be interpreted or reused, so we gate on it despite the "should".
- RA-03. S: The organization should define criteria to evaluate the significance of risk, including how consequences and likelihood are defined and measured and how the level of risk is determined; criteria should be established at the beginning of the assessment (6.3.4). I: Evaluation (RA-05) is meaningless without criteria, so we gate on criteria being present.
- RA-04. S: Risk analysis involves consideration of likelihood, consequences, and, where appropriate, the level of risk (6.4.3). I: We treat likelihood and consequence as the minimum recorded analysis. We do not mandate a scoring scale, since the standard states none (see the note on not inventing thresholds).
- RA-05. S: Risk evaluation compares the results of risk analysis with the established risk criteria to determine where additional action is required, leading to a decision (6.4.4). I: We gate on each risk carrying an evaluation decision.
- RA-06. S: Risk identification should consider sources of risk, causes and events, and consequences and their impact on objectives, among other factors (6.4.2). I: The factor list is "should be considered", so advisory.
- RA-07. S: Influences on analysis such as biases, assumptions, data quality, and limitations should be considered, documented and communicated to decision makers (6.4.3). I: Advisory quality control.
- RA-08. S: The outcome of risk evaluation should be recorded, communicated and validated at appropriate levels (6.4.4). I: Advisory, because validation is an organizational act the artefact may only reference.

### Provenance, risk-register

- RR-01. S: The risk management process and its outcomes should be documented (6.7); analysis yields likelihood, consequence, and level (6.4.3). I: We gate on the recorded outcome carrying the analysis, since a register is the record of assessed risks.
- RR-02. S: Risk treatment options include avoiding, removing the source, changing likelihood or consequence, sharing, or retaining the risk; where treatment is not available the risk should be recorded and kept under review (6.5.1, 6.5.2). I: We gate on a treatment decision or explicit acceptance per risk.
- RR-03. S: The treatment plan should identify those who are accountable and responsible for approving and implementing it (6.5.3). I: We read this as requiring an owner per risk or treatment in the register.
- RR-04. S: The remaining risk should be documented and subjected to monitoring, review and, where appropriate, further treatment (6.5.2). I: Advisory where treatment is not yet done.
- RR-05. S: The treatment plan should include rationale, those accountable and responsible, proposed actions, resources, performance measures, constraints, reporting and monitoring, and timing (6.5.3). I: Advisory, since the plan may be a separate artefact the register references.
- RR-06. S: Monitoring and review should be a planned part of the process, recording results (6.6). I: We read a maintained register as one that is dated and reviewed; advisory.

## Not-testable obligations

| Obligation (paraphrase) | Locator | Why not testable on a finished artefact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The eight risk management principles (integrated, structured, customized, inclusive, dynamic, best available information, human and cultural factors, continual improvement). | 4 | Value statements about the risk management effort as a whole, not properties of a risk assessment or register. |
| The framework: leadership and commitment, integration, design, implementation, evaluation, improvement. | 5.1 to 5.7 | Constrains the organization's risk management framework and governance, not the content of the artefact under review. |
| Communication and consultation with stakeholders throughout the process. | 6.2 | A process activity spanning the life cycle, not an inspectable property of the artefact. |
| Recording and reporting decisions should take account of information sensitivity and stakeholder needs. | 6.7 | Guidance on how to report, not a pass-or-fail property of the register. |

- `risk-register`: 3 blocking checks (RR-01 to RR-03), 3 advisory (RR-04 to RR-06).

Both constrained artefact types carry at least one blocking check (forward cross-check satisfied). Every check maps to one of the two declared artefact types, no orphans. No other normative source constrains these types, so no conflict.
