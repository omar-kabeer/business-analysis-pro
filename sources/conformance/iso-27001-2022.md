# Conformance checks: iso-27001-2022

Source id: `iso-27001-2022`
Standard: ISO/IEC 27001, Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection, Information security management systems, Requirements.
Edition held: Third edition, 2022-10 (ISO/IEC 27001:2022(E)).
Publisher: ISO/IEC.
Authority: normative.
Identity: confirmed from the title page on 2026-08-07. Third edition 2022.
Licence caveat: the copy held carries an SNV/SQS (Swiss) licence stamp and is not licensed to us. Identity is confirmed but the copy is not ours. Paraphrase only.
Modality note: ISO/IEC 27001 is a management-system requirements standard using "shall". The clauses that constrain our two artefact types are 6.1.2 (risk assessment) and 6.1.3 (risk treatment and Statement of Applicability). The wider ISMS clauses constrain the management system, not the artefact, and are recorded as not-testable.
Artefact types constrained: `security-requirement`, `compliance-matrix`.
Owning skills: `regulatory-compliance` (primary), `risk-analysis`.
Locator style: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause `<n>`.

## How to read this file

Three layers, kept separate: Source (S) with locator, labelled Inference (I), and the Check (C), answerable yes or no from the finished artefact.

## Checks: security-requirement

Applies to a stated information security requirement.

| Check id | Question (yes = pass) | Severity | Locator | How the reviewer confirms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ISO27001-SR-01 | Does the requirement trace to a risk identified by the risk assessment (a risk to confidentiality, integrity, or availability within the ISMS scope)? | blocking | 6.1.2 | Confirm the requirement references the risk it addresses. A requirement with no risk basis fails. |
| ISO27001-SR-02 | Is the requirement implemented by one or more controls determined as necessary to treat that risk? | blocking | 6.1.3 b) | Confirm the requirement maps to a control. A requirement naming no control fails. |
| ISO27001-SR-03 | Is a risk owner identified for the underlying risk, and is the residual risk approved and accepted by that owner? | blocking | 6.1.2 c), 6.1.3 f) | Confirm a named risk owner and recorded acceptance. Unowned or unaccepted residual risk fails. |
| ISO27001-SR-04 | Are applicable legal, statutory, regulatory, and contractual security requirements identified? | advisory | Annex A 5.31 | Look for identified legal and contractual requirements. Advisory. |
| ISO27001-SR-05 | Is the requirement consistent with a measurable information security objective? | advisory | 6.2 | Check alignment with a measurable objective. Advisory. |

## Checks: compliance-matrix

Applies to a Statement of Applicability or equivalent control-compliance matrix.

| Check id | Question (yes = pass) | Severity | Locator | How the reviewer confirms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ISO27001-CM-01 | Does the matrix list the necessary controls determined from risk treatment? | blocking | 6.1.3 b), d) | Confirm the matrix enumerates the controls chosen to treat the assessed risks. An empty or risk-detached matrix fails. |
| ISO27001-CM-02 | Does each listed control carry a justification for its inclusion? | blocking | 6.1.3 d) | Confirm every included control has a stated reason. A control with no justification fails. |
| ISO27001-CM-03 | Does each control record whether it is implemented or not? | blocking | 6.1.3 d) | Confirm an implementation status per control. A control with no status fails. |
| ISO27001-CM-04 | Are the controls compared against Annex A, with a justification for excluding any Annex A control? | blocking | 6.1.3 c), d) | Confirm Annex A was checked and each exclusion is justified. An unexplained Annex A omission fails. |
| ISO27001-CM-05 | Is the matrix retained as controlled, dated documented information? | advisory | 6.1.3 | Look for versioning or a date. Advisory records-management check. |

### Provenance, security-requirement

- SR-01. S: The organization shall define and apply a risk assessment process that identifies risks associated with the loss of confidentiality, integrity, and availability within the ISMS scope, and identifies the risk owners (6.1.2). I: We gate a security requirement on tracing to such a risk.
- SR-02. S: The organization shall determine all controls necessary to implement the chosen risk treatment options (6.1.3 b). I: We gate the requirement on mapping to a control.
- SR-03. S: The risk assessment identifies risk owners (6.1.2 c); the organization shall obtain risk owners' approval of the treatment plan and acceptance of residual risks (6.1.3 f). I: We gate on a named owner and recorded acceptance.
- SR-04. S: Legal, statutory, regulatory, and contractual requirements are an Annex A control area (5.31). I: Advisory.
- SR-05. S: Information security objectives shall be measurable where practicable and consistent with the policy (6.2). I: Advisory alignment.

### Provenance, compliance-matrix

- CM-01. S: The organization shall produce a Statement of Applicability that contains the necessary controls (6.1.3 d, referencing b and c). I: We treat the SoA as the compliance matrix.
- CM-02. S: The Statement of Applicability contains the justification for the controls' inclusion (6.1.3 d). I: none.
- CM-03. S: The Statement of Applicability records whether the necessary controls are implemented or not (6.1.3 d). I: none.
- CM-04. S: The organization shall compare the determined controls with Annex A and verify no necessary control is omitted, and the SoA states the justification for excluding any Annex A control (6.1.3 c, d). I: none.
- CM-05. S: The organization shall retain documented information about the risk treatment process (6.1.3). I: Advisory records-management reading.

## Not-testable obligations

| Obligation (paraphrase) | Locator | Why not testable on a finished artefact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the ISMS; context, leadership, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement clauses. | 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10 | Constrain the management system and the organization's conduct over time, not the content of a security requirement or a compliance matrix. |
| The organization shall retain documented information of the risk assessment and treatment processes. | 6.1.2, 6.1.3 | A records obligation on the organization; the artefact checks above test the SoA content, not whether the process records exist. |
