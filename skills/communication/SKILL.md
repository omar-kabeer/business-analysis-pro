# Communication Skill

## Role

Get the right business analysis information to the right people, in the right form, at the right time. Tailor the message to the audience and the decision or action it needs to prompt, and keep it clear and honest. This is the working form of the BABOK task Communicate Business Analysis Information (4.4).

## Inputs

- The information to communicate and its source (analysis, decisions, status, change).
- The audience, their level, and what they need to know or do.
- The channel and any cadence (one-off or recurring).

## Workflow

1. Fix the purpose: what the audience should know, feel, or do after reading. If there is an ask, make it explicit.
2. Fit the audience: executives want the decision and the ask first; delivery teams want the detail and the why; customers want the impact on them. The same facts get shaped differently for each. See `references/communication-formats.md`.
3. Lead with the point. Put the headline and any ask up front, then the supporting detail. Do not bury the message.
4. Choose the format: stakeholder update, status or steering report, email, announcement, or meeting notes with actions and owners.
5. Be honest about risk and status. A green status that hides a real problem destroys trust.
6. Run the natural-prose-editor pass and, for board-level material, the executive-review skill.

## Formats

Stakeholder update, status and steering committee report, project email, change announcement, and meeting notes with actions. Structures for each are in `references/communication-formats.md`.

## Outputs

- Audience-fit communication that leads with the point and states any ask.
- Recurring report formats with a consistent structure.
- Meeting notes with decisions, actions, and owners.

## Quality gates

- The purpose and any ask are clear within the first few lines.
- The message is tailored to the audience's level and need.
- Status and risk are stated honestly, not softened into a false green.
- The language follows the house style with no em dashes.

## Done when

The message is tailored, leads with its point, states any ask, and is honest about status and risk.

## House style

Run the natural-prose-editor skill as the final language pass. No em dashes. See `docs/methodology/editorial-style.md`.
