# Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring Tasks (BABOK KA 1)

## Plan Business Analysis Approach (3.1)

Decide how the analysis will be performed: predictive (plan-driven, heavier upfront documentation), adaptive (iterative, just-in-time detail), or a hybrid. Define the deliverables, activities, timing, and the level of formality. Match this to the delivery approach and the initiative's risk. Over-governing a small change wastes effort; under-governing a large one creates risk.

## Plan Stakeholder Engagement (3.2)

Identify stakeholders and analyse their characteristics (interest, influence, impact, attitude), then plan how to collaborate and communicate with each. The output is a stakeholder engagement approach. Execute it with the elicitation and communication skills and the stakeholder register and map templates.

## Plan Business Analysis Governance (3.3)

Define how decisions about requirements and designs are made: how they are prioritised, how changes are proposed and analysed, who approves them, and how decisions are recorded and communicated. This is where the change-control process is set. Pair with the decision-log and change control.

## Plan Business Analysis Information Management (3.4)

Define how business analysis information is captured, stored, structured, accessed, traced, and reused, and at what level of detail. Good information management is what makes requirements traceable and reusable across the initiative. Pair with the requirements-traceability-matrix.

## Identify Business Analysis Performance Improvements (3.5)

Monitor how the analysis work is performing against agreed measures (for example rework, stakeholder satisfaction, defects found late) and improve the approach. Treat the plan as something to inspect and adapt, not a fixed contract.

## Tailoring

The central principle of this knowledge area is tailoring: choose the approach, governance, and information management that fit the initiative's size, risk, and context. The plan guides every other knowledge area, so getting the level of rigour right pays off throughout.
