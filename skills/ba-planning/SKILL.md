# Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring Skill

## Role

Plan and monitor the business analysis effort itself, so the work is organised, governed, and proportionate to the initiative. This is BABOK knowledge area 1. Its outputs guide the tasks in every other knowledge area. Tailor the rigour to the initiative rather than applying a fixed process.

## Inputs

- The initiative, its size, risk, and delivery approach (predictive, adaptive, or hybrid).
- Stakeholders and their engagement needs.
- Organisational standards, governance, and constraints.

## Workflow

See `references/ba-planning-tasks.md` for the knowledge-area tasks.

1. Plan the business analysis approach: how analysis will be performed, the deliverables, activities, timing, and level of rigour, matched to the delivery approach.
2. Plan stakeholder engagement: identify stakeholders, analyse their characteristics, and plan collaboration and communication. (Use the elicitation and communication skills to execute.)
3. Plan business analysis governance: how requirements and design decisions are prioritised, changed, and approved, and who decides. Define the change-control process.
4. Plan business analysis information management: how analysis information is stored, accessed, traced, and reused, at the right level of detail.
5. Identify performance improvements: monitor how the analysis work is performing and improve the approach.

## Outputs

- A business analysis approach and plan proportionate to the initiative.
- A stakeholder engagement plan.
- A governance approach with a change-control process.
- An information management and traceability approach.

## Quality gates

- The approach fits the initiative's size, risk, and delivery method; it is not one-size-fits-all.
- Governance defines who decides, how changes are proposed and approved, and how they are recorded.
- Information management keeps requirements traceable and reusable at the right level of detail.
- The plan is monitored and adjusted, not written once and ignored.

## Done when

The analysis approach, stakeholder engagement, governance, and information management are planned and proportionate, and a way to monitor and improve the work is in place.
