# Normative sources for the vendor-evaluation skill

Conformance authorities, not citation sources. Judge an artefact by whether it passes the checks, not by whether it cites the standard. Do not quote the standards in deliverables.

Resolve each source id against `sources/manifest.json` and read its checks in `sources/conformance/<source-id>.md`.

## vendor-assessment

- `iso-20400`. ISO 20400:2017, sustainable procurement guidance. A vendor assessment must pass the ISO20400-VA blocking checks: it evaluates suppliers against the defined sustainability criteria and requirements, and it considers each supplier's capacity to meet those requirements, including through the supply chain. Advisory checks cover matching the evaluation procedure to non-conformity risk and consistent, fair application across suppliers. Note: ISO 20400 is guidance ("should"), so the blocking severities are our house gate.
