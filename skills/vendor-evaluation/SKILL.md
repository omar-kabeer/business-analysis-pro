# Vendor Evaluation Skill

## Role

Run a fair, transparent, and defensible selection of a supplier or product against the organisation's needs. Turn requirements into weighted criteria, gather comparable information, score consistently, and recommend with the trade-offs visible.

## Inputs

- The need and the requirements the solution must meet (from the requirements skill).
- Constraints: budget, timeline, must-haves, and regulatory or security needs.
- Candidate vendors or products, if known.

## Workflow

1. Define the requirement and separate must-haves (pass or fail) from weighted, scored criteria. Cover functional fit, non-functional needs (security, scalability, support), commercial terms, viability of the vendor, and risk. See `references/vendor-evaluation-methods.md`.
2. Choose the instrument: an RFI to gather information and longlist, then an RFP to get detailed, comparable proposals from a shortlist.
3. Agree the scoring scale and weights before seeing responses, so the process is not tuned to a favoured vendor.
4. Score each vendor consistently against the criteria, using demos or a proof of concept where capability claims need testing. Hand total-cost-of-ownership comparison to the finance skill.
5. Produce the evaluation report: scores, the reasoning, risks, references or due-diligence findings, and a clear recommendation with the runner-up and why.

## Techniques

Vendor assessment, RFI and RFP structuring, weighted scoring matrices, criteria design, and demo or proof-of-concept scoring. Details in `references/vendor-evaluation-methods.md`.

## Outputs

- Weighted evaluation criteria with must-haves separated.
- RFI or RFP content and a completed scoring matrix.
- An evaluation report with a recommendation and the trade-offs.

## Quality gates

- Must-haves are separated from scored criteria; scoring scale and weights are set before responses are seen.
- Every vendor is scored against the same criteria with the same evidence standard.
- Cost is compared on total cost of ownership, not headline price.
- The recommendation is defensible, with risks and the runner-up stated.

## Done when

Vendors are scored transparently against agreed weighted criteria and a defensible recommendation with trade-offs is delivered.
