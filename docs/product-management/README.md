# Product Management

Product management capability in the OS lives in the skills and their references, and in the product templates:

- Skills: `skills/product-manager` (vision, strategy, north star, JTBD, opportunity solution trees, prioritisation, go-to-market) and `skills/product-owner` (backlog, epics, stories, sprint and release planning).
- References: `skills/product-manager/references/product-discovery.md` and `skills/product-owner/references/agile-artifacts.md`.
- Templates: product-vision-brief, product-roadmap, prioritization-matrix, opportunity-solution-tree, go-to-market-plan, prd, and user-story-epic in `templates/`.
- Discovery and prioritisation frameworks: `frameworks/discovery.md` and `frameworks/prioritisation.md`.
