# Worked Example: Supplier Invoice Approval Automation

A second end-to-end example, taken through the full life cycle and its decision gates using `docs/methodology/sdlc-playbook.md`. Where the first example (customer self-service portal) shows discovery to a BRD, this one shows the whole arc: feasibility to a funded decision, requirements, and a release-readiness gate, then post-launch evaluation.

## The flow and gates

- Inception and discovery: the request in `scenario.md`.
- Strategy and feasibility, investment gate: `business-case.md` (funded decision).
- Requirements, readiness-to-build gate: `user-stories.md` (INVEST stories with acceptance criteria).
- Test and release, release gate: `release-readiness.md` (a completed release-readiness checklist with a go decision).
- Operate and evaluate: `evaluation.md` (the business case graded, plus a post-launch value note).

## What it demonstrates

The OS routes an unstructured request through the skills, produces the right deliverable at each gate, and validates the output. It also shows the three control gates from the SDLC playbook: invest, build, release.
