## Post-launch value note (solution evaluation)

After launch, the solution-evaluation skill would measure the outcomes against the business case: approval cycle time, erroneous payment rate, discount capture, and cost per invoice. Early low adoption would be examined as a possible enterprise limitation (process or training) before assuming a solution defect. Any shortfall becomes a new business need feeding the next cycle.
