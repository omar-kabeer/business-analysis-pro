# Change Strategy

## Purpose

Describe the approach to move from the current state to the future state, chosen from alternatives and justified. This is the working form of the Change Strategy output of the BABOK Define Change Strategy task (6.4). Define the transition states along the way.

## Inputs

The current state description, the future state description, and the risk analysis results.

## 1. Context of the change

Describe the nature and context of the change.

## 2. Alternatives considered

Identify the alternative change strategies and compare them.

| Alternative | Advantages | Disadvantages | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |

## 3. Recommended strategy and justification

State the chosen strategy and why it is the best approach.

## 4. Investment and resources

State the investment and resources required to reach the future state.

## 5. Value realization

Describe how the enterprise realizes value after the solution is delivered.

## 6. Transition states

Define the transition states along the way, each showing what is complete and what value is realized.

| Transition state | Solution parts complete | Value realized |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |

## 7. Key stakeholders

Identify the key stakeholders in the change.

## 8. Risks

Record the risks to the change with responses and owners. Evaluate a completed strategy with `evaluation/change-strategy-rubric.md`.
