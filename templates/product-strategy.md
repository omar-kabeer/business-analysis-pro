# Product Strategy Document

## Purpose

Define how the product wins: who it serves, how it is positioned, the value it delivers, how it makes money, the strategic bets, and how each bet is defensible. It sits between the vision (the multi-year destination) and the roadmap (the sequenced path). Grounded in modern product strategy, including Gibson Biddle's DHM lens.

## 4. Revenue model

Describe how the product captures value: pricing model (freemium, tiered, usage-based), packaging, and the unit economics that matter (CAC, LTV, gross margin).

## 5. Strategic bets

The two to four bets the strategy rests on. Test each against the DHM lens.

| Bet | Delight (customer pain solved) | Hard-to-copy advantage (which moat) | Margin (profit or funding effect) | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |  |

Moats to consider: network effects, economies of scale, brand equity, unique technology, counter-positioning, switching costs, process power.

## 6. Where we will not play

State the deliberate exclusions that keep the strategy focused.

## Review criteria

- Segments are prioritised and tied to a job to be done.
- Positioning is specific and differentiated.
- The revenue model and unit economics are stated.
- Every strategic bet passes the DHM test (delight, defensibility, or margin), avoiding the feature factory.
- The strategy connects the vision to the roadmap.
