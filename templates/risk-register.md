# Risk Register

## Purpose

Identify, assess, and manage the risks to an initiative in a structured, auditable way: describe each risk, score its probability and impact on a consistent scale, choose a response, assign an owner, and track residual risk over time. This is the working form of BABOK Risk Analysis and Management (10.38).

## Scoring scales

Score probability and impact on a consistent 1 to 5 scale. Severity is probability times impact (1 to 25). Agree the scale up front so scores are comparable across risks.

| Score | Probability | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rare | Negligible |
| 2 | Unlikely | Minor |
| 3 | Possible | Moderate |
| 4 | Likely | Major |
| 5 | Almost certain | Severe |

Response strategies: avoid (remove the source), reduce (mitigate probability or impact), transfer (share with another party), or accept (tolerate, with a contingency).

## Review criteria

- Each risk states cause, event, and effect and is scored on the agreed scale.
- Every risk has a response, actions, an owner, and a review date.
- Residual risk after the response is recorded, not just inherent risk.
- The register is reviewed on a stated cadence and severity trends are tracked.
