# Product Vision Brief

## Purpose

State why the product exists, who it serves, the outcome it targets, and how it wins, on a single page that aligns the team and stakeholders. The vision is outcome-first and durable; it guides strategy and roadmap without prescribing features.

## 6. Horizon outlook

A brief now, next, later view of how the product evolves toward the vision. Keep it at the level of outcomes.

## 7. What we are not doing

State the deliberate exclusions that keep the product focused.

## Review criteria

- The vision is outcome-first, durable, and customer-centred.
- The north-star metric captures real customer value and is measurable.
- Strategic pillars connect clearly to the vision.
- Non-goals are explicit.
