# Information Management Approach

## Purpose

Define how business analysis information is stored, accessed, reused, and maintained on this initiative. Business analysis information covers requirements, designs, elicitation results, solution options, and change strategy, not requirements alone. This is the working form of the BABOK Plan Business Analysis Information Management task (3.4) and its output, the Information Management Approach.

## Inputs

The business analysis approach, the governance approach, and the stakeholder engagement approach.

## 1. Scope of information

List the types of business analysis information the approach governs, including elicitation results, designs, and solution options.

## 2. Storage and access

State where each type of information is stored and who can access it under what conditions.

## 3. Traceability

Describe how relationships and traceability between items are captured and maintained.

## 4. Reuse

State how information is named, structured, and stored so it can be found and reused beyond the current change.

## 5. Status and change

Describe how the repository indicates the status of each item and supports change over time.

## 6. Retention and archival

Set out retention and archival rules, and the policy each rule derives from.

## 7. Tools

State the tools used and how they shape capture, notation, and organization.

## 8. Security and access control

Describe how access rights are set and how sensitive information is protected.

## 9. Review and agreement

Record who agreed the approach and when. Evaluate a completed approach with `evaluation/information-management-approach-rubric.md`.
