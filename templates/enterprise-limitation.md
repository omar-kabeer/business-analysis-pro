# Enterprise Limitation

## Purpose

Describe the factors external to the solution that are restricting value realization. This is the working form of the BABOK Assess Enterprise Limitations task (8.4) and its output, the Enterprise Limitation.

## Inputs

The current state description, the implemented solution, and the solution performance analysis.

## 1. External limitations

Record each external factor, the readiness dimension it belongs to, its impact on value, and its owner.

## 2. Readiness summary

Summarise the enterprise's readiness to realize the value of the solution, and what the limitations imply for action. Evaluate a completed assessment with `evaluation/enterprise-limitation-rubric.md`.
