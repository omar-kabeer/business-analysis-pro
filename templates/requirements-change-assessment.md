# Requirements Change Assessment

## Purpose

Assess a proposed change to requirements and recommend whether to approve, modify, or deny it. This is the working form of the BABOK Assess Requirements Changes task (5.4) and its output, the Requirements Change Assessment. Run the assessment through the change-control process defined in the governance approach.

## Inputs

The proposed change, the affected requirements, and the governance approach.

## 1. Proposed change

Describe the change requested, its source, and the reason for it.

## 2. Impact analysis

Assess the effect on requirements, coverage, and allocation.

## 3. Cost, benefit, and risk

Weigh the cost of making the change, the benefit it delivers, and the risk it carries.

## 4. Stakeholders consulted

List the stakeholders consulted, including the sponsor and tester.

## 5. Recommendation

State the recommendation to approve, modify, or deny, and the justification that follows from the analysis. Evaluate a completed assessment with `evaluation/requirements-change-assessment-rubric.md`.
