# Requirements Architecture

## Purpose

Structure the requirements of the initiative and the interrelationships among them, so the set can be understood and assessed as a whole. This is the working form of the BABOK Define Requirements Architecture task (7.4) and its output, the Requirements Architecture.

## Inputs

The requirements in any state, the information management approach, and the solution scope.

## 1. Structure

Describe how the requirements are organized, for example by level, by capability, or by viewpoint.

## 2. Relationships

Record the interrelationships among requirements.

## 3. Viewpoints

List the viewpoints represented and the models or frameworks used for each.

## 4. Completeness check

State how the architecture is used to confirm the requirements are complete, and note any gap found. Evaluate a completed architecture with `evaluation/requirements-architecture-rubric.md`.
