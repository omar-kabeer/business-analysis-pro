# Empathy Map

## Purpose

Build shared understanding of a user before designing, by capturing what they say, think, do, and feel, plus their pains and gains. Pairs with the persona and journey map. Grounded in the UX artifacts reference.

## Review criteria

- The map reflects evidence about a real segment, not assumptions presented as fact.
- Says, thinks, does, and feels are distinct and specific.
- Pains and gains point to design opportunities.
