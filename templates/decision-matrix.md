# Weighted Decision Matrix

## Purpose

Make the basis of a choice visible: the options considered, the criteria applied, the weights agreed before scoring, the evidence behind each score, and how robust the answer is.

## Constraints applied

| Constraint | Mandatory | Options eliminated | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Options failing a mandatory requirement are eliminated, not scored down.

## Criteria and weights

| Criterion | Derived from which objective | Weight | Reason for the weight | Independent of other criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Weights are set and recorded before any score is seen. Total weight equals 100.

## Scale

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Does not meet the need |
| 3 | Meets the need |

## Uncertainty

Where outcomes are uncertain, record probability and outcome per option, the expected value, and whether a one-off downside makes expected value the wrong guide.
