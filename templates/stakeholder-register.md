# Stakeholder Register

## Purpose

Record every group or individual with a relationship to the change, the need, or the solution, together with the characteristics that decide how each one is engaged. This register is the working form of the BABOK Stakeholder Engagement Approach (Plan Stakeholder Engagement, 3.2) and the Stakeholder List technique (10.43). A thorough register lowers the risk that a source of requirements is missed later.

## Scope

State which change or initiative this register covers and the boundary used to decide who is in scope. Include anyone affected by, influencing, or interacting with the solution, inside and outside the organisation.

## Inputs

Solution or change scope, organisational models and org charts, job descriptions and procedure manuals, the business need, and prior stakeholder lists. Brainstorming and interviews are common ways to build the list.

## Stakeholder characteristics captured

For each stakeholder record their role, their level of authority in the domain and the organisation, their interest in and attitude toward the change, their decision-making authority, and how the change affects them. These are the characteristics BABOK calls for when analysing stakeholders.

Guidance for the columns:

- BABOK role: use the generic roles where they fit (customer, domain subject matter expert, end user, implementation subject matter expert, operational support, project manager, regulator, sponsor, supplier, tester) and add others as needed. One person can hold several roles.
- Interest and Influence: rate High, Medium, or Low. These two drive the stakeholder matrix in the stakeholder map and RACI template.
- Attitude: current stance toward the change, used to plan engagement, not to judge the person.
- Engagement approach: how you will involve them (co-design, consult, inform, monitor) consistent with their interest and influence.

## Assumptions

List anything taken as true when building the register (for example, that the org chart is current) and flag it for confirmation.

## Risks

An incomplete register is the main risk: a missed stakeholder means missed or late requirements and rework. Review the list for gaps across every affected group before closing discovery.

## Review criteria

- The list is exhaustive for the stated scope and no affected group is missing.
- Each entry has role, interest, influence, and an engagement approach.
- Characteristics are evidence-based or marked as an assumption.
- Terminology matches the project glossary.

## BABOK anchor

Plan Stakeholder Engagement (3.2); Stakeholder List, Map, or Personas (10.43). Feeds the stakeholder map and RACI template and the elicitation plan.
