# Elicitation Activity Plan

## Purpose

Plan an elicitation activity: its desired outcomes, the techniques to use, the participants, and the logistics. This is the working form of the BABOK Prepare for Elicitation task (4.1) and its output, the Elicitation Activity Plan. Plan only what the activity needs, and match the techniques to the outcomes and the stakeholders.
