# Process Model

## Purpose

Document a business process so it is visible, analysable, and ready to improve or specify. Captures the boundary (SIPOC), the current-state flow, the analysis, and the future-state flow. Based on BABOK Process Modelling (10.35) and Process Analysis (10.34).

## Review criteria

- The process has a clear trigger, outcome, and boundary (SIPOC).
- Roles and handoffs are explicit; swimlanes are used where handoffs matter.
- Analysis names root causes, not just symptoms, with evidence where possible.
- The future state states what changes and the benefit.
