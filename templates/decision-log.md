# Decision Log

## Purpose

Record significant decisions with enough context that a newcomer can understand not just what was decided but why, and under what conditions it would be revisited. A good decision log prevents relitigating settled questions and makes governance auditable.

Guidance:

- Options considered: list the real alternatives, including the one chosen, so the trade-off is visible.
- Rationale: the reasoning, the evidence, and the assumptions behind the choice.
- Reversal conditions: what new information or change would trigger revisiting the decision.

## Review criteria

- Each decision records the options, the rationale, and the decision maker.
- The date and status are present.
- Reversal conditions are stated where the decision is likely to be revisited.
- Superseded decisions are marked and linked, not deleted.
