# Force Field Analysis Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a force field analysis weighs the forces for and against a change soundly. A force field analysis identifies the driving and restraining forces acting on a change. Used by the strategy and change-control skills. Based on BABOK force field analysis practice.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Driving forces | The forces pushing the change forward are identified. |
| 2 | Restraining forces | The forces resisting the change are identified. |
| 3 | Weighting | The relative strength of each force is assessed. |
| 4 | Evidence | The forces and their weights are evidenced, not guessed. |
| 5 | Balance | The net balance of forces is drawn out. |
| 6 | Actions | Actions to strengthen drivers or weaken restraints are proposed. |
| 7 | Actionable | The analysis supports a decision or plan. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the strategy skill.
