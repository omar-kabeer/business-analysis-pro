# Requirements (validated) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether requirements have been validated to deliver benefit. Validated requirements and designs can be demonstrated to deliver benefit to stakeholders and to align with the business goals and objectives of the change. Used by the quality and requirements skills. Based on BABOK Validate Requirements (7.3).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Benefit demonstrated | Each requirement can be shown to deliver benefit to stakeholders. |
| 2 | Goal alignment | Each aligns with the business goals and objectives of the change. |
| 3 | Value traceable | The value of each requirement traces to a business objective or need. |
| 4 | Stakeholder agreement | Stakeholders agree that their needs are met by the requirements. |
| 5 | Assumptions tested | The assumptions behind the expected benefit are tested. |
| 6 | Non-benefiting removed | Requirements that cannot be validated are challenged or removed. |
| 7 | Measures | The measures that will confirm the benefit are identified. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the requirements skill.
