# Stakeholder Engagement Approach Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a stakeholder engagement approach is ready to guide collaboration and communication on an initiative. The approach plans how the team works with stakeholders and includes the stakeholder analysis results and the risks to engagement. Used by the elicitation and quality skills and applied to any approach produced from `templates/stakeholder-engagement-approach.md`. Based on BABOK Plan Stakeholder Engagement (3.2).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Stakeholder coverage | All affected stakeholders and groups are identified, with no material group missed. |
| 2 | Analysis | Influence, interest, attitude, and authority are assessed for each stakeholder or group. |
| 3 | Collaboration fit | The engagement and collaboration method is matched to each stakeholder. |
| 4 | Communication | What is communicated, to whom, when, and in what format and channel is defined. |
| 5 | Needs and expectations | Stakeholder needs and expectations of the effort are captured. |
| 6 | Cadence | The frequency of engagement and the triggers for it are defined. |
| 7 | Decisions and authority | Decision rights and approval roles across the stakeholder set are clear. |
| 8 | Risks | Risks to engagement such as availability, resistance, or conflict are identified with responses. |
| 9 | Fit to the approach | The engagement approach is consistent with the business analysis approach and the governance approach. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 27):

- Pass: 22 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 16 to 21, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 16, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
