# SWOT Matrix Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a SWOT matrix is evidence-backed and actionable. A SWOT matrix sets out strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. Used by the strategy skill. Based on BABOK SWOT Analysis (10.46).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Four quadrants | Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats are all populated. |
| 2 | Internal and external correct | Strengths and weaknesses are internal; opportunities and threats are external. |
| 3 | Evidence-backed | Each entry is supported by evidence, not assertion. |
| 4 | Specific | Entries are specific, not generic filler. |
| 5 | MECE | Entries are placed in the right quadrant, with minimal overlap. |
| 6 | Balanced | The matrix is candid across all four quadrants. |
| 7 | Actionable | The matrix supports strategy choices, for example pairing strengths to opportunities. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the strategy skill.
