# Stakeholder Register Evaluation Rubric

Assess a Stakeholder Register built from `templates/stakeholder-register.md`, against BABOK Plan Stakeholder Engagement (3.2) and Stakeholder List, Map, or Personas (10.43). It judges whether the register finds everyone the change touches and records enough about each to plan their engagement. The BABOK-named `stakeholder-list-stakeholder-map-personas-rubric.md` remains for the combined technique work product.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Coverage | Every group affected by, influencing, or interacting with the change is listed, checked against the BABOK roles and the process, with no known gap left unowned. |
| 2 | Characteristics | Each entry records role, interest, influence, impact of the change, attitude, decision authority, and key needs. |
| 3 | Evidence basis | Each entry says whether its characteristics are evidenced (and from where) or assumed, and assumptions have a plan to confirm them. |
| 4 | Map placement | Placement on the interest and influence map follows from the recorded ratings. |
| 5 | Engagement fit | Each stakeholder has an engagement approach and communication plan that fit their position and needs. |
| 6 | Personas | Where personas are used, they rest on research and link to the register entries they represent. |
| 7 | Gaps and risks | Coverage gaps and stakeholder risks, such as a resistant group with influence, have an owner, a response, and a date. |

## Scoring anchors

Anchors describe the extreme scores; 1 and 2 interpolate between them.

| # | Score 0 (absent) | Score 3 (strong) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Only managers and the project team are listed; affected users, operations, or regulators are missing. | Every group affected by, influencing, or interacting with the change is listed, checked against the BABOK roles and the process, with no known gap left unowned. |
| 2 | Entries are names only, with no characteristics. | Each entry records role, interest, influence, impact of the change, attitude, decision authority, and key needs. |
| 3 | Attitudes and influence are guesses presented as fact. | Each entry says whether its characteristics are evidenced (and from where) or assumed, and assumptions have a plan to confirm them. |
| 4 | No map, or placements that contradict the ratings. | Placement on the interest and influence map follows from the recorded ratings. |
| 5 | One engagement approach for everyone, or none. | Each stakeholder has an engagement approach and communication plan that fit their position and needs. |
| 6 | Personas are invented archetypes with no evidence or link. | Where personas are used, they rest on research and link to the register entries they represent. |
| 7 | Gaps and resistance are known but not recorded or owned. | Coverage gaps and stakeholder risks, such as a resistant group with influence, have an owner, a response, and a date. |

## Common failure modes

- A register copied from the org chart without asking who else is affected.
- Suppliers, customers, or regulators left out because they are outside the organisation.
- Attitude recorded as a judgement of the person rather than their stance on the change.
- A register built once at kickoff and never revisited.

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or 17 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 12.

Dimensions 1 (coverage) and 2 (characteristics) are blocking: a score of 0 on either fails the document whatever the total. Record findings by severity with specific fixes, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill. A draft that does not pass may still be useful; its quality state must remain visible.
