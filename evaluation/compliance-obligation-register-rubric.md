# Compliance Obligation Register Evaluation Rubric

Assess a Compliance Obligation Register against `templates/compliance-obligation-register.md`, the BABOK treatment of legal and regulatory information as a guideline for requirements, Business Rules Analysis (10.9), and Trace Requirements (5.1). The register must let compliance be demonstrated, not asserted, so an obligation with no requirement, control, or evidence counts as a gap. This rubric judges analysis quality; it is not a legal opinion. Used by the regulatory-compliance skill.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Scope and as-at date | Entities, jurisdictions, products, and data categories in scope are stated, exclusions are explicit, and the register carries an as-at date so its currency is known. |
| 2 | Obligation completeness | Every applicable law, regulation, standard, and internal policy is listed at clause level, with the obligation restated as what it actually demands. |
| 3 | Obligation to requirement trace | Each obligation traces to at least one requirement ID, and no requirement claims a compliance basis the register does not hold. |
| 4 | Control design | Each obligation has named controls with a control type, and the mix includes detective or corrective controls where prevention alone could fail silently. |
| 5 | Evidence and frequency | Each control names the evidence that proves it operates and how often that evidence is produced, so an auditor could request it. |
| 6 | Gap handling | Every obligation not fully covered appears as a gap with residual risk, interim mitigation, owner, target date, and a matching risk register ID. |
| 7 | Conflicts and interpretation | Conflicting or ambiguous clauses record the reading taken, who holds authority for it, and whether it was referred to counsel. |
| 8 | Personal data and review | Where personal data is in scope, lawful basis, minimisation, retention and its source, residency, transfers, and subject rights are recorded, and the review history is current. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or 20 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 14.

Record findings by severity with specific fixes. Route material issues back to the regulatory-compliance skill for revision. An artefact that does not pass may still be useful as a draft; its quality state must remain visible.
