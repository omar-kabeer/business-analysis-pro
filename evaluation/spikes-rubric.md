# Spikes Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a spike is well-scoped and answers its question. A spike is a time-boxed investigation to reduce uncertainty about an approach or estimate. Used by the agile-coach and product-owner skills. Based on BABOK Agile Perspective (11.1).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Question defined | The question the spike answers is stated. |
| 2 | Time-boxed | The spike has a time box. |
| 3 | Outcome defined | The expected output, such as a decision or estimate, is defined. |
| 4 | Answer produced | The spike produces an answer to its question. |
| 5 | Evidence | The answer is backed by what the spike found. |
| 6 | Disposable | Any code or model built is treated as a means, not a deliverable. |
| 7 | Feeds backlog | The result feeds the backlog or the next decision. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the agile-coach skill.
