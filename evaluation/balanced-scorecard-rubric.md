# Balanced Scorecard Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a balanced scorecard links strategy to measures across perspectives. A balanced scorecard sets objectives, measures, and targets across the financial, customer, internal process, and learning and growth perspectives. Used by the strategy and business-intelligence skills. Based on BABOK Balanced Scorecard (10.3).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Four perspectives | The financial, customer, internal process, and learning and growth perspectives are all covered. |
| 2 | Objectives | Each perspective carries strategic objectives. |
| 3 | Measures | Each objective has a measure that reflects it. |
| 4 | Targets | Each measure has a target. |
| 5 | Strategy alignment | The objectives and measures align with the strategy. |
| 6 | Cause and effect | The links between perspectives, such as learning driving process, are drawn. |
| 7 | Balanced | The scorecard is not dominated by financial measures alone. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the strategy skill.
