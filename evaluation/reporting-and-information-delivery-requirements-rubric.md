# Reporting and Information-Delivery Requirements Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether reporting and information-delivery requirements are complete and measurable. These requirements define what information must be delivered, to whom, in what form, and how often. Used by the business-intelligence and requirements skills. Based on BABOK Business Intelligence Perspective (11.2).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Audience | The audience for each report or delivery is named. |
| 2 | Content | The information each audience needs is defined. |
| 3 | Delivery mode | Standard, ad-hoc, and analytic delivery needs are distinguished. |
| 4 | Format and channel | The format, channel, and frequency are stated. |
| 5 | Measurable | The requirements are specific enough to build and test against. |
| 6 | Timeliness | Latency and refresh needs are stated. |
| 7 | Traceable | The requirements trace to objectives and data sources. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the business-intelligence skill.
