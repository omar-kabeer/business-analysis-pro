# Metrics and KPIs Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a set of metrics and key performance indicators will measure what matters. A metric is a quantifiable level of an indicator used to measure progress; a KPI measures progress toward a strategic goal. Used by the data-analysis and business-intelligence skills. Based on BABOK Metrics and Key Performance Indicators (10.28).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Valid indicators | Each metric is a valid, quantifiable indicator of progress. |
| 2 | Aligned | Each aligns to a goal, objective, or output that matters to stakeholders. |
| 3 | KPI distinction | The KPIs measure progress toward strategic goals specifically. |
| 4 | Baseline and target | A baseline starting point and a target metric are defined. |
| 5 | Collectable | A data-collection method exists and is affordable. |
| 6 | Reporting | The reporting format, recipients, and interval are defined. |
| 7 | Structure | The monitoring and evaluation structure of collection, analysis, and reporting is defined. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the data-analysis skill.
