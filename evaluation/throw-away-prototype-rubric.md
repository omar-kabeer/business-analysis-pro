# Throw-away (rapid) Prototype Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a throw-away prototype answers its question quickly and is then discarded. A throw-away, or rapid, prototype is built fast to answer a specific question and is not carried into the solution. Used by the prototyping and ux skills. Based on BABOK Prototyping (10.36).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Question targeted | The specific question the prototype answers is stated. |
| 2 | Fast | It was built quickly, matching its throw-away purpose. |
| 3 | Answers the question | The prototype produces the answer it was built for. |
| 4 | Disposable | It is understood to be discarded, not evolved into the solution. |
| 5 | Findings captured | The findings are captured before the prototype is discarded. |
| 6 | Right fidelity | The fidelity is only as high as the question needs. |
| 7 | Next step | The decision the finding informs is identified. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the prototyping skill.
