# Collaborative Game Outputs Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether the outputs of a collaborative game achieved shared understanding. Collaborative games, such as product box or affinity mapping, produce shared understanding and agreed outputs. Used by the elicitation skill. Based on BABOK Collaborative Games (10.10).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Objective met | The game achieved the objective it was run for. |
| 2 | Participation | The right participants took part and contributed. |
| 3 | Shared understanding | The output reflects a shared understanding, not one voice. |
| 4 | Captured | The outputs are captured clearly for later use. |
| 5 | Themes | Where the game produces groupings, they are meaningful and labelled. |
| 6 | Actionable | The outputs feed the next step of the work. |
| 7 | Human-led | The facilitation is acknowledged as human-led, with its judgment recorded. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
