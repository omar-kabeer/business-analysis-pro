# Acceptance and Evaluation Criteria Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether acceptance and evaluation criteria are measurable and usable. Acceptance criteria define the minimum a solution must meet; evaluation criteria define measures for ranking options. Used by the quality and solution-evaluation skills. Based on BABOK Acceptance and Evaluation Criteria (10.1).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Testable | Each criterion is expressed so it can be verified as met or not met. |
| 2 | Measurable | Evaluation criteria carry a measurable scale, not a vague adjective. |
| 3 | Coverage | The criteria cover the requirements or value attributes they judge. |
| 4 | Unambiguous | Each criterion has a single, clear pass condition. |
| 5 | Value-based | The criteria are grounded in value attributes that matter to stakeholders. |
| 6 | Agreed | The criteria are agreed with the relevant stakeholders. |
| 7 | Weighted | Where evaluation criteria are used to rank options, their weighting is justified. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the quality skill.
