# Enterprise Readiness Assessment Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether an enterprise readiness assessment is thorough. The assessment describes whether the enterprise is prepared to accept the change and able to use the solution effectively. Used by the change-control and strategy skills. Based on BABOK Define Change Strategy (6.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Dimensions covered | Cultural, operational, technical, and stakeholder readiness are all considered. |
| 2 | Current readiness | The current level of readiness is assessed with evidence. |
| 3 | Gaps | The gaps to the readiness the change needs are identified. |
| 4 | Impact | The impact of each gap on adoption and value is stated. |
| 5 | Actions | Actions to reach readiness are proposed. |
| 6 | Owned | The actions are owned and scheduled. |
| 7 | Ability to absorb | The enterprise's capacity to absorb the change is judged. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the change-control skill.
