# Solution Limitation Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a solution limitation is described soundly enough to act on. A solution limitation describes the current limitations of the solution, including its constraints and defects, that restrict value realization. Used by the solution-evaluation and quality skills and applied to limitations produced from `templates/solution-limitation.md`. Based on BABOK Assess Solution Limitations (8.3).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Limitations identified | The current limitations of the solution are described. |
| 2 | Constraints and defects | Constraints and defects are captured. |
| 3 | Evidence | The limitations are evidenced, not asserted. |
| 4 | Value impact | The impact on value realization is quantified where possible. |
| 5 | Root cause | The cause of each limitation is understood. |
| 6 | Traceable | The limitations tie to the solution performance analysis. |
| 7 | Actionable | The limitations are stated so that actions can address them. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the solution-evaluation skill.
