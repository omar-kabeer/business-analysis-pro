# Artefact Register Evaluation Rubric

Assess an Artefact Register against `templates/artefact-register.md` and BABOK Plan Business Analysis Information Management (3.4) and Maintain Requirements (5.2). The register is the index that makes every other artefact findable, so it is judged on whether someone new to the initiative could locate, trust, and reuse each item from it alone. Used by the information-management skill.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Coverage | Every artefact the initiative has produced or committed to produce is listed, including elicitation records, models, requirements, designs, and governance records, with no known item missing. |
| 2 | Identity and conventions | Every row carries a unique ID that follows the stated ID pattern, names follow the naming convention, and IDs of superseded items are never reused. |
| 3 | Classification | Each artefact has a class and a type (artifact, work product, or deliverable), and only deliverables are marked as needing formal acceptance. |
| 4 | Ownership and location | Each artefact has a named owner and a resolvable location in the stated repository, so it can be found and questioned without asking around. |
| 5 | Version and status currency | Current version, status, and last-updated date are present and agree with the artefact itself; superseded rows point to their replacement. |
| 6 | Baseline integrity | Each baseline lists the exact IDs and versions it contains, who approved it, when, and under what conditions, so the baseline can be reconstructed. |
| 7 | Single source of truth | Recurring questions each map to one authoritative artefact, and overlapping artefacts are merged or demoted to linked views. |
| 8 | Access, retention, and reuse | Read and write access, retention rules with their source, archive actions, and reuse candidates with a currency check are recorded for each class. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or 20 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 14.

Record findings by severity with specific fixes. Route material issues back to the information-management skill for revision. An artefact that does not pass may still be useful as a draft; its quality state must remain visible.
