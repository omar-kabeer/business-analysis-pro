# Purpose Alignment Model Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a purpose alignment model classifies activities against strategy soundly. The model classifies activities by how mission-critical and how market-differentiating they are, to guide investment. Used by the strategy skill. Based on BABOK Agile Perspective (11.1).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Dimensions correct | Activities are classified on mission-critical and market-differentiating axes. |
| 2 | Activities placed | Each activity is placed in the correct quadrant. |
| 3 | Evidence | The placements are evidenced, not asserted. |
| 4 | Investment implication | The model draws the investment implication of each quadrant, such as differentiate or partner. |
| 5 | Strategy aligned | The classification aligns to the enterprise strategy. |
| 6 | Complete | The relevant activities are covered. |
| 7 | Actionable | The model supports a decision about where to invest. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the strategy skill.
