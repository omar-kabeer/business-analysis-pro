# Lessons Learned Document / Register Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a lessons learned record is useful for future work. A lessons learned register captures what went well and what did not, with recommendations. Used by the governance and ba-planning skills. Based on BABOK Lessons Learned (10.27).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Successes and failures | Both what went well and what did not are captured. |
| 2 | Specific | Each lesson is specific, not a platitude. |
| 3 | Root cause | The cause behind each lesson is understood. |
| 4 | Recommendations | Each lesson carries an actionable recommendation. |
| 5 | Owned | Recommendations for future work are owned. |
| 6 | Balanced | The record is candid, not a blame or a whitewash. |
| 7 | Reusable | The lessons are stored so future initiatives can find them. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the ba-planning skill.
