# Designs (approved) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether designs are approved soundly enough for work to proceed. Approved designs have been agreed by the stakeholders responsible for approving them, so solution construction can continue. Used by the governance and quality skills. Based on BABOK Approve Requirements (5.5).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Agreement obtained | The right stakeholders have agreed to the designs. |
| 2 | Authority | The approvers hold the decision-making authority defined in the governance approach. |
| 3 | Conflicts resolved | Consensus was sought and conflicts were managed before approval. |
| 4 | Communication | The designs were communicated clearly to the approvers. |
| 5 | Finalized | The approved designs are a finalized work product ready to build. |
| 6 | Traceable | The approval is recorded and traceable to the requirements the designs satisfy. |
| 7 | Governance fit | The approval follows the defined approval process. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the governance skill.
