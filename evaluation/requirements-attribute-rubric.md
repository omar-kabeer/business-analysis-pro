# Requirements Attribute Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether the attributes recorded against requirements are complete and useful. Requirements attributes are metadata about a requirement, such as priority, source, status, and owner. Used by the requirements and information-management skills. Based on BABOK requirements attributes practice.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Core attributes | Priority, source, status, and owner are captured for each requirement. |
| 2 | Consistency | The attribute values are used consistently across requirements. |
| 3 | Current | The attributes are kept current as the requirement changes. |
| 4 | Defined values | The permitted values for each attribute are defined. |
| 5 | Traceability | Source and related-requirement attributes support traceability. |
| 6 | Complete | No requirement is missing an attribute the process relies on. |
| 7 | Useful | The attributes support prioritization, change control, and reporting. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the requirements skill.
