# Onion Diagram Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether an onion diagram places stakeholders correctly by proximity to the solution. An onion diagram arranges stakeholders in concentric rings around the solution, from the delivery team out to affected external parties. Used by the elicitation and visual-modelling skills. Based on BABOK Stakeholder List, Map, or Personas (10.43).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rings correct | The rings represent the right levels: solution, affected unit, organization, and affected external parties. |
| 2 | Coverage | All relevant stakeholders and groups appear on the diagram. |
| 3 | Placement | Each stakeholder is placed in the ring that matches their proximity to the solution. |
| 4 | Groups named | Stakeholder groups are named clearly rather than lumped together. |
| 5 | Consistency | The placement is consistent with the stakeholder analysis. |
| 6 | No omissions | No affected external party, such as a regulator or customer, is missed. |
| 7 | Readable | The diagram is legible and labelled so a reader can use it unaided. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
