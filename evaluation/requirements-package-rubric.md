# Requirements Package Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a requirements package is ready to serve as a baseline. A requirements package, also called a requirements document, is a specialized business analysis package concerned with requirements and may represent a baseline of a collection of requirements. Used by the requirements and quality skills. Based on BABOK Packaging and the Glossary.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Baseline complete | The collection of requirements is complete for its scope. |
| 2 | Requirement quality | The packaged requirements meet the verify characteristics: atomic, unambiguous, testable, and the rest. |
| 3 | Consistent | There are no conflicting requirements across the package. |
| 4 | Traceable | The requirements trace to their source and to each other. |
| 5 | Versioned | Version and status are recorded. |
| 6 | Approved and baselined | Where a baseline is intended, the package is reviewed and agreed as a reference point. |
| 7 | Audience fit | The format suits the consumers of the package. |
| 8 | Change control | The package is under change control. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 14, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the requirements skill.
