# Data Mining Model / Findings Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether data mining findings are valid and reproducible. Data mining produces a model or findings that surface patterns and insights in data. Used by the data-analysis and business-intelligence skills. Based on BABOK Data Mining (10.14).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Question defined | The question or objective the mining addresses is stated. |
| 2 | Data quality | The data used is described, with its quality and limitations. |
| 3 | Method valid | The technique is appropriate to the data and the question. |
| 4 | Findings significant | The patterns found are statistically or practically significant, not noise. |
| 5 | Reproducible | The method is documented well enough to reproduce. |
| 6 | Interpreted | The findings are interpreted in business terms, not left as raw output. |
| 7 | Actionable | The insights support a decision or action. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the data-analysis skill.
