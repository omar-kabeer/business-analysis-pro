# Business Analysis Performance Assessment Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a business analysis performance assessment is sound enough to drive improvement. The assessment compares business analysis work against performance objectives and recommends improvements. Used by the quality and ba-planning skills and applied to any assessment produced from `templates/business-analysis-performance-assessment.md`. Based on BABOK Identify Business Analysis Performance Improvements (3.5).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Objectives and metrics | The performance objectives and the metrics or KPIs used to assess the work are defined and appropriate. |
| 2 | Evidence | The assessment is backed by data and observation, not opinion. |
| 3 | Analysis against objectives | Actual performance is compared to the objectives and the gaps are identified. |
| 4 | Root causes | The causes of any shortfall are analysed, not just the symptoms. |
| 5 | Recommended actions | Preventive, corrective, or improvement actions are proposed. |
| 6 | Actionability | Recommendations are specific, owned, and feasible. |
| 7 | Impact on the approach | Resulting changes to the business analysis approach, processes, and tools are identified. |
| 8 | Stakeholder input | The assessment draws on relevant stakeholder feedback. |
| 9 | Follow-through | How the actions will be tracked to closure is stated. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 27):

- Pass: 22 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 16 to 21, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 16, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the ba-planning and quality skills.
