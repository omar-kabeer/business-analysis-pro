# Business Analysis Information (communicated) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether business analysis information has been packaged and communicated so stakeholders share a common understanding. Used by the communication and quality skills and applied to any business analysis information package produced from `templates/business-analysis-information-communicated.md`. Based on BABOK Communicate Business Analysis Information (4.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Purpose clear | The objective of the communication is defined. |
| 2 | Audience fit | The package suits what each audience understands and needs. |
| 3 | Format and channel | An appropriate format and channel are chosen for the audience. |
| 4 | Clarity | The information is conveyed clearly and in a usable form. |
| 5 | Completeness | The package contains what the audience needs, no more and no less. |
| 6 | Obligations | Any regulatory or contractual constraints on the communication are met. |
| 7 | Supports next activity | The package enables the downstream activity it feeds. |
| 8 | Shared understanding | The communication confirms that stakeholders share a common understanding. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 14, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the communication skill.
