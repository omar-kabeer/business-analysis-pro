# Governance Approach Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a governance approach is ready to control how decisions are made on an initiative. The approach defines how requirements and designs are approved, prioritised, and changed, and who holds each decision. Used by the governance and quality skills and applied to any approach produced from `templates/governance-approach.md`. Based on BABOK Plan Business Analysis Governance (3.3).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Decision rights | Who decides what is explicit, and the decision-making process is defined. |
| 2 | Approval process | Who approves requirements and designs, and at which checkpoints, is stated. |
| 3 | Change control | How changes are requested, assessed, and approved or denied is defined. |
| 4 | Prioritisation | The approach and rules for prioritising requirements are set out. |
| 5 | Roles | The stakeholders in each governance role are named, in a RACI-style allocation with no gaps. |
| 6 | Escalation | How disagreements and issues escalate and resolve is defined. |
| 7 | Policy alignment | The approach aligns with organizational policy and any legal or regulatory constraint. |
| 8 | Baselining and versioning | How baselines are set, versioned, and maintained is defined. |
| 9 | Fit and agreement | The approach is consistent with the business analysis approach and agreed by stakeholders. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 27):

- Pass: 22 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 16 to 21, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 16, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the governance skill.
