# Cost-Benefit Analysis Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a cost-benefit analysis is credible. A cost-benefit analysis quantifies the costs and benefits of an option to support a decision. Used by the finance and strategy skills. Based on BABOK Financial Analysis (10.20).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Costs quantified | The costs are identified and quantified, including ongoing costs. |
| 2 | Benefits quantified | The benefits are identified and quantified where possible. |
| 3 | Assumptions | The assumptions behind the figures are stated and sourced. |
| 4 | Horizon | A sensible time horizon is used and stated. |
| 5 | Discounting | Where relevant, future cash flows are discounted, with NPV or ROI computed correctly. |
| 6 | Intangibles | Intangible costs and benefits are recorded, even if not monetized. |
| 7 | Conclusion | The conclusion follows honestly from the numbers. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the finance skill.
