# Elicitation Results (confirmed) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether confirmed elicitation results are trustworthy enough to commit resources to using them. Confirmed elicitation results have been checked against their source and other information and agreed as accurate. Used by the elicitation and quality skills and applied to elicitation results after confirmation. Based on BABOK Confirm Elicitation Results (4.3).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Checked for accuracy | The results have been reviewed for accuracy and consistency. |
| 2 | Compared to source | The results were compared against their source and other elicitation results. |
| 3 | Conflicts resolved | Errors, omissions, conflicts, and ambiguity were identified and resolved. |
| 4 | Stakeholder agreement | The results are confirmed with the relevant stakeholders. |
| 5 | Traceable | Each result traces to its source and to the need. |
| 6 | Complete | Gaps found during confirmation are closed before use. |
| 7 | Ready for use | The results are fit to feed the downstream tasks that consume them. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
