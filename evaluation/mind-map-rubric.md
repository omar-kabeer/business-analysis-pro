# Mind Map Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a mind map organizes a topic clearly. A mind map arranges a central topic and its branches to show structure and associations. Used by the elicitation and visual-modelling skills. Based on BABOK Mind Mapping (10.29).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Central topic | The central topic is clear. |
| 2 | Branches logical | The main branches are logical and distinct. |
| 3 | Coverage | The map covers the topic without a major gap. |
| 4 | Associations | Sub-branches and cross-links show meaningful associations. |
| 5 | Depth | The map goes to a useful depth without sprawling. |
| 6 | Labels | Nodes are labelled concisely. |
| 7 | Readable | The map is legible and can be followed unaided. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
