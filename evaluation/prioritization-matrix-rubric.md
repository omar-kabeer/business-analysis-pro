# Prioritization Matrix Evaluation Rubric

Assess the whole Prioritization Matrix against `templates/prioritization-matrix.md` and the BABOK Prioritization technique (10.33). Scores such as Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort are estimates and must be presented as such; do not reward invented usage data or measured results that were never collected.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Method fit | One primary method (RICE, Value vs Effort, Kano, MoSCoW, or WSJF) is chosen and it fits the type of decision being made. |
| 2 | Consistent application | The chosen method and its scale are applied the same way to every item in the decision, not mixed or changed partway through. |
| 3 | Scoring transparency | The scales and definitions behind each score (for example what Reach, Impact, or Confidence mean here) are stated so another reviewer could reproduce the ranking. |
| 4 | Item completeness | The set of items being ranked is the relevant candidate set for the decision, not a partial or pre-selected list that begs the question. |
| 5 | Outcome alignment | The ranking ties back to the stated target outcome rather than to request order, seniority, or recency. |
| 6 | Defensible output | A final rank or decision (do now, plan, fill-in, avoid, or Must, Should, Could, Won't) is explicit and follows from the recorded scores. |
| 7 | Evidence honesty | Reach, impact, confidence, and effort are labelled as estimates or assumptions where they are not measured; no score is presented as observed data it is not. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 18 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 17, or 18 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 12.

Record findings by severity with specific fixes. A matrix may be useful for discussion even when it does not pass; its quality state must remain visible.
