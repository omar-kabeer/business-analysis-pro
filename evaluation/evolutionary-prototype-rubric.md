# Evolutionary (functional) Prototype Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether an evolutionary prototype is a sound working increment. An evolutionary, or functional, prototype is a working build that is extended over time toward the solution. Used by the prototyping and quality skills. Based on BABOK Prototyping (10.36).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Working increment | The prototype works for the increment it covers. |
| 2 | Extensible | It is built to be extended rather than discarded. |
| 3 | Requirements traceable | The functionality traces to the requirements it realizes. |
| 4 | Quality baseline | It meets a quality bar suitable for building on, not throwaway code. |
| 5 | Scope clear | What is and is not yet built is clear. |
| 6 | Feedback captured | User feedback on the increment is captured and fed back. |
| 7 | Path forward | The next increment and how it extends this one are defined. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the prototyping skill.
