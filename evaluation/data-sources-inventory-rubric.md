# Data Sources Inventory Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a data sources inventory is complete and reliable. The inventory catalogues the data sources available to a solution, with their characteristics. Used by the business-intelligence and information-management skills. Based on BABOK Business Intelligence Perspective (11.2).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sources listed | The relevant data sources are identified, with none material missed. |
| 2 | Characteristics | Each source records its owner, format, refresh, and access method. |
| 3 | Quality noted | The quality and reliability of each source are noted. |
| 4 | Coverage | The inventory covers the data the solution needs. |
| 5 | Ownership | Each source has a named owner or steward. |
| 6 | Sensitivity | Sensitivity and access constraints are recorded. |
| 7 | Current | The inventory is current. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the business-intelligence skill.
