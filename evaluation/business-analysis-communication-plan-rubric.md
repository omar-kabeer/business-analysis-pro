# Business Analysis Communication Plan Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging a business analysis communication plan. The plan describes the types, audience, and frequency of business analysis communication. It is a BABOK v2 artefact; in BABOK v3 this is covered by the stakeholder engagement approach, so prefer that for new work. Used by the communication skill.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Audience | The audiences for communication are identified. |
| 2 | Content | What is communicated to each audience is defined. |
| 3 | Frequency | The frequency and triggers for communication are stated. |
| 4 | Format and channel | The format and channel for each communication are chosen. |
| 5 | Responsibilities | Who communicates what is clear. |
| 6 | Feedback | How feedback is gathered and acted on is defined. |
| 7 | Currency | Where the BABOK v3 stakeholder engagement approach exists, the plan defers to it. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the communication skill.
