# Stakeholder List, Map, or Personas Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a stakeholder list, map, or set of personas identifies and characterizes stakeholders soundly. These artefacts identify the stakeholders and represent their characteristics, influence, and needs. Used by the elicitation skill. Based on BABOK Stakeholder List, Map, or Personas (10.43).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Coverage | All relevant stakeholders and groups are identified. |
| 2 | Characteristics | Influence, interest, and needs are captured for each. |
| 3 | Map correct | Where a map is used, stakeholders are placed correctly by influence or proximity. |
| 4 | Personas evidenced | Where personas are used, they are grounded in evidence, not invented. |
| 5 | Goals and pain points | Personas capture goals and pain points, not just demographics. |
| 6 | Representative | The set represents the real stakeholder population. |
| 7 | Feeds engagement | The artefact informs the stakeholder engagement approach. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
