# Process Performance Assessment Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a process performance assessment draws sound conclusions. The assessment analyses process performance against goals and identifies where and why it falls short. Used by the process-modelling and business-analysis skills. Based on BABOK Business Process Management Perspective (11.5).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Against goals | Performance is assessed against the process goals and measures. |
| 2 | Evidence | The assessment is based on measured data, not impression. |
| 3 | Gaps identified | The gaps between actual and target performance are identified. |
| 4 | Drivers | The drivers and root causes of the gaps are analysed. |
| 5 | Conclusions supported | The conclusions follow from the evidence. |
| 6 | Recommendations | Improvements are recommended for the gaps found. |
| 7 | Traceable | The assessment ties to the process measures and objectives. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the process-modelling skill.
