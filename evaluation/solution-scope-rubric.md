# Solution Scope Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a solution scope defines the solution boundary clearly enough to steer delivery. The solution scope defines the boundaries of the solution and shows how it enables the future state's goals. Used by the strategy and quality skills and applied to any solution scope produced from `templates/solution-scope.md`. Based on BABOK Define Change Strategy (6.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Boundaries clear | The in-scope boundary is defined, so stakeholders understand which new capabilities the change delivers. |
| 2 | Capabilities | The scope is described through capabilities, functions, processes, data, or other appropriate views. |
| 3 | Enables future state | The scope shows how the proposed solution enables the future state's goals. |
| 4 | Out of scope | Out-of-scope components are stated for clarity. |
| 5 | Detail level | The scope is described in enough detail for stakeholders to act, without over-specifying. |
| 6 | Alignment | The scope is consistent with the chosen change strategy and the future state. |
| 7 | Unambiguous | There is no ambiguity about what is in and what is out. |
| 8 | Evolvable | The scope is treated as able to evolve as more is discovered. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 14, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the strategy skill.
