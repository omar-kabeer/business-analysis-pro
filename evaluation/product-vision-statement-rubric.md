# Product Vision Statement Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a product vision statement is clear and directional. A product vision statement expresses the intent and value of a product to guide the work. Used by the product-manager skill. Based on BABOK Agile Perspective (11.1).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Target customer | The target customer or user is named. |
| 2 | Need | The need or problem the product addresses is stated. |
| 3 | Value | The core value and differentiation are clear. |
| 4 | Concise | The vision is concise and memorable. |
| 5 | Aligned | The vision aligns to the enterprise strategy. |
| 6 | Directional | The vision gives direction that can steer roadmap and backlog. |
| 7 | Measurable intent | The outcomes that would show the vision is met are indicated. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the product-manager skill.
