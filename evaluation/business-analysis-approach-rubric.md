# Business Analysis Approach Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a business analysis approach is fit to guide the work on an initiative. The approach describes the overall method for the business analysis effort: how and when tasks are performed and the deliverables produced. Used by the ba-planning and quality skills and applied to any approach produced from `templates/business-analysis-approach.md`. Based on BABOK Plan Business Analysis Approach (3.1).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Fit to context | The approach is matched to the initiative's type, complexity, and life cycle, whether predictive, adaptive, or hybrid. |
| 2 | Activities and deliverables | The business analysis tasks, work products, and deliverables to be produced are defined. |
| 3 | Timing and sequencing | When tasks are performed and how they sequence with the wider project or delivery approach is set out. |
| 4 | Techniques | An initial set of techniques is selected and justified, with room to adjust as understanding grows. |
| 5 | Roles and responsibilities | Who performs the work, and who is consulted or approves, is clear. |
| 6 | Tailoring | Where a standard process was tailored, the changes and their rationale are stated. |
| 7 | Complexity and estimation | The effort and complexity of the work are considered and the approach is scaled accordingly. |
| 8 | Stakeholder agreement | The approach is agreed with the appropriate stakeholders. |
| 9 | Risks to the approach | Risks to performing the business analysis work are identified with responses. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 27):

- Pass: 22 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 16 to 21, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 16, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the ba-planning skill.
