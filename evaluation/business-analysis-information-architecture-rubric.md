# Business Analysis Information Architecture Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether the structure of business analysis information is sound. The business analysis information architecture defines how business analysis information is structured and organized so it is accessible and usable. Used by the information-management skill. Based on BABOK Plan Business Analysis Information Management (3.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Structure defined | The organization of the information is defined. |
| 2 | Coverage | It covers the range of business analysis information, not just requirements. |
| 3 | Accessible | The structure makes information findable and accessible to those who need it. |
| 4 | Relationships | The relationships between information types are represented. |
| 5 | Consistent | The structure is consistent with the information management approach. |
| 6 | Maintainable | The architecture can accommodate change over time. |
| 7 | Traceable | The structure supports traceability across the information. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the information-management skill.
