# Prioritized List / Ranking Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a prioritized list ranks items soundly. A prioritized list ranks items by their relative importance using a stated method. Used by the requirements and product-owner skills. Based on BABOK Prioritization (10.33).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Method explicit | The prioritization method, such as MoSCoW or value, is stated. |
| 2 | Criteria | The criteria driving priority are clear. |
| 3 | Consistent | The method is applied consistently across all items. |
| 4 | Dependencies | Dependencies between items are reflected in the ranking. |
| 5 | Stakeholder-agreed | The priorities are agreed with the relevant stakeholders. |
| 6 | Value focus | The ranking aims at maximizing value. |
| 7 | Revisable | The list can be re-prioritized as the context changes. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the requirements skill.
