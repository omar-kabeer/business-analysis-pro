# Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a work breakdown structure decomposes the work soundly. A WBS is a hierarchical decomposition of the total work to be done. Used by the estimation and ba-planning skills. Based on BABOK Functional Decomposition and estimation practice.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | MECE | The breakdown is mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive at each level. |
| 2 | 100 percent rule | The children of each node sum to the whole of that node, with no missing or extra work. |
| 3 | Leaf nodes estimable | The lowest-level work packages are small enough to estimate and assign. |
| 4 | Deliverable oriented | The structure is organized around deliverables or outcomes, not only activities. |
| 5 | Consistent decomposition | Each branch is decomposed to a consistent and appropriate level. |
| 6 | Traceable | The work packages trace to the scope and deliverables. |
| 7 | Usable | The structure supports estimation, scheduling, and assignment. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the estimation skill.
