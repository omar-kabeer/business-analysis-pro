# Executive Summary Evaluation Rubric

Assess an Executive Summary against `templates/executive-summary.md`, BABOK Communicate Business Analysis Information (4.4), and Recommend Solution (7.6). The summary is judged on whether a senior reader can make the decision from it alone and whether every figure in it survives comparison with the full document. Used by the executive-review skill, with the deliverable-critic agent as an independent check.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Bottom line up front | The recommendation and the specific ask appear in the first two sentences, so the reader could stop there and know what to approve. |
| 2 | Situation and complication | The context and the reason a decision is needed now are stated briefly and accurately, without background that does not change the decision. |
| 3 | Structured support | The case beneath the recommendation is grouped into a few mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive points. |
| 4 | Quantified value and cost | Benefit, investment, and timeline are quantified with a time frame and a stated level of confidence. |
| 5 | Options and trade-offs | The main alternatives are named with the reason each lost, so the recommendation is shown to be a choice. |
| 6 | Risk candour | The principal risks and assumptions are stated with how they are managed, and the downside is not buried. |
| 7 | Consistency with source | Every figure, date, and claim matches the full document it summarises and its visuals. |
| 8 | Decision clarity and length | The decision required names the approvers, conditions, and deadline, and the whole summary fits about one page in plain language. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or 20 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 14.

Record findings by severity with specific fixes. Route material issues back to the executive-review skill for revision. An artefact that does not pass may still be useful as a draft; its quality state must remain visible.
