# Enterprise Limitation Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether an enterprise limitation is described soundly enough to act on. An enterprise limitation describes the factors external to the solution that are restricting value realization. Used by the solution-evaluation and quality skills and applied to limitations produced from `templates/enterprise-limitation.md`. Based on BABOK Assess Enterprise Limitations (8.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | External factors | The factors external to the solution that restrict value are identified. |
| 2 | Coverage | Culture, operational, and stakeholder readiness factors are considered. |
| 3 | Evidence | The limitations are evidenced, not asserted. |
| 4 | Value impact | The impact on value realization is quantified where possible. |
| 5 | Owned | Each limitation has a responsible owner. |
| 6 | Traceable | The limitations tie to the solution performance analysis and the objectives. |
| 7 | Actionable | The limitations are stated so that actions can address them. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the solution-evaluation skill.
