# Documented Interview Responses / Notes Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether interview notes capture the session accurately and usably. Interview notes record the questions asked and the responses given during an interview. Used by the elicitation skill. Based on BABOK Interviews (10.25).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Questions relevant | The questions asked were relevant to the elicitation goal. |
| 2 | Responses accurate | The responses are captured accurately and attributed. |
| 3 | Context | The interviewee, date, and context are recorded. |
| 4 | Follow-ups | Follow-up questions and clarifications are captured. |
| 5 | Open items | Unanswered questions and actions are noted. |
| 6 | Confirmable | The notes are clear enough to confirm with the interviewee. |
| 7 | Traceable | The responses can be traced to needs or requirements. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
