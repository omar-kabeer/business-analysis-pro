# Dashboards Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a dashboard presents the right measures to its audience clearly. A dashboard presents metrics and indicators for monitoring and decision-making. Used by the business-intelligence and data-analysis skills. Based on BABOK Business Intelligence Perspective (11.2).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Metrics valid | The metrics shown are valid and aligned to the objectives they support. |
| 2 | Audience fit | The dashboard suits the audience and the decisions they make. |
| 3 | Prioritised | The most important measures are prominent, not buried. |
| 4 | Drill-down | Where needed, the dashboard supports drill-down from summary to detail. |
| 5 | Accurate | The figures are accurate and their source and refresh are clear. |
| 6 | Context | Targets, baselines, or trends give the numbers context. |
| 7 | Readable | The layout is clear and not cluttered. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the business-intelligence skill.
