# Solution Recommendations Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a solution recommendation is defensible. The recommendation establishes which design option is most appropriate to meet the enterprise's requirements, based on the potential value of each. Used by the solution-evaluation and quality skills and applied to recommendations produced from `templates/solution-recommendation.md`. Based on BABOK Analyze Potential Value and Recommend Solution (7.6).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Value estimated | The potential value of each design option is estimated. |
| 2 | Comparison | The options are compared on value, advantages, and disadvantages. |
| 3 | Uncertainty | The uncertainty in the estimates is acknowledged. |
| 4 | Most appropriate | The recommended option is the most appropriate to meet the enterprise's requirements. |
| 5 | Justification | The recommendation is defensible and traces to the evaluation criteria. |
| 6 | Value dimensions | Value is described across finance, reputation, or market impact as relevant. |
| 7 | Feasible | The recommended course is achievable. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the solution-evaluation skill.
