# Epics Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether an epic is well-formed and ready to decompose. An epic is a large body of work that is broken down into features and stories. Used by the product-owner and agile-coach skills. Based on BABOK Agile Perspective (11.1).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Outcome framed | The epic is framed by the outcome or value it delivers. |
| 2 | Scope clear | The scope of the epic and what it excludes are clear. |
| 3 | Decomposable | The epic can be broken into features and stories. |
| 4 | Acceptance | High-level acceptance criteria or a definition of done are stated. |
| 5 | Valuable | The epic delivers value to a user or the business. |
| 6 | Prioritised | The epic has a priority relative to others. |
| 7 | Traceable | The epic traces to a goal, theme, or product vision. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the product-owner skill.
