# Solution Performance Measures Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether solution performance measures will reveal how a solution is performing. Performance measures are the metrics and indicators that quantify how the solution is performing against its goals. Used by the solution-evaluation and business-intelligence skills and applied to measures produced from `templates/solution-performance-measures.md`. Based on BABOK Measure Solution Performance (8.1).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reflect performance | The measures best reflect the performance of the solution. |
| 2 | Aligned | They align with the business objectives and enterprise measures. |
| 3 | Baseline and target | A baseline and a target are defined for each. |
| 4 | Collectable | The data can be collected practically and affordably. |
| 5 | Valid | The measures are valid indicators, not proxies that mislead. |
| 6 | KPIs identified | Key performance indicators for the strategic goals are identified. |
| 7 | Reporting | How and when the measures are reported is defined. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the solution-evaluation skill.
