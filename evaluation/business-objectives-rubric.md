# Business Objectives Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a set of business objectives is clear and measurable enough to steer a future state. Business objectives describe the desired direction the business wishes to pursue to achieve the future state. Used by the strategy and quality skills and applied to business objectives captured for an initiative. Based on BABOK Define Future State (6.2). Applied to objectives produced from `templates/business-objectives.md`, and to OKR sets from `templates/okrs.md`.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Outcome focused | Each objective describes a desired outcome or direction, not a solution. |
| 2 | Measurable | Each is specific and measurable, with a metric rather than a vague aspiration. |
| 3 | Target | Each carries a target and, where useful, a baseline. |
| 4 | Strategy alignment | The objectives align with enterprise strategy and the underlying need. |
| 5 | Future-state link | The objectives support the defined future state. |
| 6 | Achievable | Each is realistic given the known constraints. |
| 7 | Owned | A sponsor or owner is accountable for each objective. |
| 8 | Prioritised | The relative importance of the objectives is clear. |

## Scoring anchors

Anchors describe the extreme scores; 1 and 2 interpolate between them.

| # | Score 0 (absent) | Score 3 (strong) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Objectives name solutions or activities, not outcomes. | Each objective describes a desired outcome or direction, not a solution. |
| 2 | Objectives are aspirations with no measure. | Each is specific and measurable, with a metric rather than a vague aspiration. |
| 3 | No target or baseline. | Each carries a target and, where useful, a baseline. |
| 4 | No link to strategy or the business need. | The objectives align with enterprise strategy and the underlying need. |
| 5 | No link to the future state. | The objectives support the defined future state. |
| 6 | Targets were never checked against constraints. | Each is realistic given the known constraints. |
| 7 | No owner, or a committee as owner. | A sponsor or owner is accountable for each objective. |
| 8 | All objectives are equally important. | The relative importance of the objectives is clear. |

## Common failure modes

- "Implement a new workflow tool" written as an objective.
- Targets with no baseline, so progress cannot be shown.
- Measures nobody collects.
- Objectives owned by the project rather than the business.
- Five objectives with no rule for trading them off.

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or 20 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 14.

Dimensions 1 (outcome focused) and 2 (measurable) are blocking: a score of 0 on either fails the document whatever the total. Record findings by severity with specific fixes, and route material issues back to the strategy skill. A draft that does not pass may still be useful; its quality state must remain visible.
