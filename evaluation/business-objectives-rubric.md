# Business Objectives Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a set of business objectives is clear and measurable enough to steer a future state. Business objectives describe the desired direction the business wishes to pursue to achieve the future state. Used by the strategy and quality skills and applied to business objectives captured for an initiative. Based on BABOK Define Future State (6.2).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Outcome focused | Each objective describes a desired outcome or direction, not a solution. |
| 2 | Measurable | Each is specific and measurable, with a metric rather than a vague aspiration. |
| 3 | Target | Each carries a target and, where useful, a baseline. |
| 4 | Strategy alignment | The objectives align with enterprise strategy and the underlying need. |
| 5 | Future-state link | The objectives support the defined future state. |
| 6 | Achievable | Each is realistic given the known constraints. |
| 7 | Owned | A sponsor or owner is accountable for each objective. |
| 8 | Prioritised | The relative importance of the objectives is clear. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 14, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the strategy skill.
