# Interview Guide Evaluation Rubric

Assess the whole Interview Guide (plan, capture log, and confirmation) against `templates/interview-guide.md` and the BABOK Interviews technique (10.25). Only what the interviewer actually captured counts as observed; a prepared question set is a plan, not a finding, and must not be reported as if it were answered.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Objective and preparation | The interview objective, interviewee, and interview type (structured or unstructured) are stated, and background was reviewed so the session does not cover what is already known. |
| 2 | Question quality | Questions are open, non-leading, and asked one at a time, moving from current state to pain to goals to other stakeholders to edge cases to priority. |
| 3 | Coverage of the objective | The question set and any probes map back to the stated objective, so the interview could plausibly inform the decision it was meant for. |
| 4 | Fact and opinion separation | Captured statements distinguish fact from opinion and attribute each point to the interviewee as the source. |
| 5 | Assumptions and unknowns | Assumptions and unknowns surfaced during the interview are recorded explicitly, with the impact if wrong and who can confirm them. |
| 6 | Actions and follow-ups | Follow-up actions have an owner and a due date, so nothing raised in the interview is lost. |
| 7 | Confirmation of results | Key points were summarised back to the interviewee to confirm accuracy, matching the Confirm Elicitation Results step (4.3), before the findings were used. |
| 8 | Evidence honesty | The record shows what the interviewee actually said, not what the interviewer expected to hear; unanswered questions and open items are marked as such rather than filled in. |

## Scoring anchors

Anchors describe the extreme scores; 1 and 2 interpolate between them.

| # | Score 0 (absent) | Score 3 (strong) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | No objective or type; the interviewer arrived unprepared. | The interview objective, interviewee, and interview type (structured or unstructured) are stated, and background was reviewed so the session does not cover what is already known. |
| 2 | Questions are leading, closed, or stacked, so answers confirm what the interviewer wanted. | Questions are open, non-leading, and asked one at a time, moving from current state to pain to goals to other stakeholders to edge cases to priority. |
| 3 | Questions wander away from the objective. | The question set and any probes map back to the stated objective, so the interview could plausibly inform the decision it was meant for. |
| 4 | Opinions are recorded as facts, with no attribution. | Captured statements distinguish fact from opinion and attribute each point to the interviewee as the source. |
| 5 | Assumptions and unknowns are filled in with guesses or not recorded. | Assumptions and unknowns surfaced during the interview are recorded explicitly, with the impact if wrong and who can confirm them. |
| 6 | Follow-ups have no owner or date. | Follow-up actions have an owner and a due date, so nothing raised in the interview is lost. |
| 7 | Findings were used without being confirmed with the interviewee. | Key points were summarised back to the interviewee to confirm accuracy, matching the Confirm Elicitation Results step (4.3), before the findings were used. |
| 8 | The record reports what the interviewer expected, or fills unanswered questions. | The record shows what the interviewee actually said, not what the interviewer expected to hear; unanswered questions and open items are marked as such rather than filled in. |

## Common failure modes

- A questionnaire read aloud, with no probes.
- Questions that name the solution, such as "Would a portal help?"
- Notes that paraphrase so heavily the interviewee would not recognise them.
- One interview treated as the voice of a whole group.
- No confirmation step, so misunderstandings reach the requirements.

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or 20 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 14.

Dimensions 2 (question quality) and 8 (evidence honesty) are blocking: a score of 0 on either fails the document whatever the total. Record findings by severity with specific fixes, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill. A draft that does not pass may still be useful; its quality state must remain visible.
