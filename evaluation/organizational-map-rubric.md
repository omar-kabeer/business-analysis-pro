# Organizational Map Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether an organizational map represents how units interact soundly. An organizational map shows the organizational units and how they interact, distinct from a reporting org chart. Used by the business-architecture skill. Based on BABOK Business Architecture Perspective (11.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Units identified | The organizational units in scope are identified. |
| 2 | Interactions | The interactions and relationships between units are shown. |
| 3 | Not a reporting chart | The map shows interaction, not just reporting lines. |
| 4 | Boundaries | Internal and external boundaries are clear. |
| 5 | Complete | The units and interactions relevant to the purpose are covered. |
| 6 | Aligned | The map aligns to capabilities and value streams. |
| 7 | Readable | The map is legible and can be used unaided. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the business-architecture skill.
