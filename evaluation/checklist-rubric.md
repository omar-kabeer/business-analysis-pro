# Checklist Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a quality-control checklist is fit for verifying work. A checklist is a set of quality items used to verify requirements, designs, or other work products. Used by the quality skill. Based on BABOK Verify Requirements (7.2).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Coverage | The items cover the quality criteria the checklist is meant to enforce. |
| 2 | Unambiguous items | Each item has an unambiguous pass or fail condition. |
| 3 | Atomic | Each item checks one thing. |
| 4 | Relevant | The items are relevant to the work product being checked. |
| 5 | Ordered | The items are ordered so the check flows logically. |
| 6 | Maintainable | The checklist can be updated as standards change. |
| 7 | Usable | A reviewer can apply the checklist consistently. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the quality skill.
