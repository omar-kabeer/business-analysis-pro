# Elicitation Activity Plan Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether an elicitation activity plan is ready to run. The plan defines the desired outcomes, techniques, participants, and logistics for elicitation. Used by the elicitation and quality skills and applied to any plan produced from `templates/elicitation-activity-plan.md`. Based on BABOK Prepare for Elicitation (4.1).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Desired outcomes | The outcomes and the work products the elicitation must produce are defined. |
| 2 | Techniques | The techniques selected suit those outcomes and the stakeholders involved. |
| 3 | Stakeholders | The right participants are identified and their availability is considered. |
| 4 | Scope | The scope and purpose of each activity are clear. |
| 5 | Logistics | Schedule, location, and resources are arranged. |
| 6 | Supporting materials | The materials needed are identified and procured. |
| 7 | Collaboration | The conditions that foster collaboration during the activity are considered. |
| 8 | Fit | The plan is consistent with the business analysis approach and the stakeholder engagement approach. |

## Scoring anchors

Anchors describe the extreme scores; 1 and 2 interpolate between them.

| # | Score 0 (absent) | Score 3 (strong) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | No outcomes; the activity is scheduled for its own sake. | The outcomes and the work products the elicitation must produce are defined. |
| 2 | Techniques are chosen by habit, with no link to outcomes or people. | The techniques selected suit those outcomes and the stakeholders involved. |
| 3 | Participants are whoever is available, not whoever knows. | The right participants are identified and their availability is considered. |
| 4 | Scope is unstated, so sessions sprawl. | The scope and purpose of each activity are clear. |
| 5 | No schedule, place, or resources. | Schedule, location, and resources are arranged. |
| 6 | Materials are not identified until the day. | The materials needed are identified and procured. |
| 7 | Nothing is done to help people take part openly. | The conditions that foster collaboration during the activity are considered. |
| 8 | The plan ignores the BA approach and the engagement approach. | The plan is consistent with the business analysis approach and the stakeholder engagement approach. |

## Common failure modes

- A workshop for everything, whatever the question.
- Managers and their staff in one session, so staff stay quiet.
- Month-end sessions with finance teams.
- A plan that never names the work product the results feed.
- No fallback when key participants are unavailable.

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or 20 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 14.

Dimensions 1 (desired outcomes) and 2 (techniques) are blocking: a score of 0 on either fails the document whatever the total. Record findings by severity with specific fixes, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill. A draft that does not pass may still be useful; its quality state must remain visible.
