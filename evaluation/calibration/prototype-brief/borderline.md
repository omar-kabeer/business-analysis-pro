# Prototype Brief: Approver Screen

Question: can approvers find what to check and approve an invoice easily? Decision: whether this layout goes into build.
Type: clickable mock-up, because layout is the main uncertainty.
Scope: invoice summary, match status, approve and reject.
Traces to: STORY-018, STORY-019.
Session: 5 approvers, including 2 who have not used the pilot, asked to approve an invoice; consent recorded.
Findings: generally positive; two asked where the PO number was.
Disposition: discard after the second round.
