# Business Objectives: Supplier Invoice Approval

Scope: UK accounts payable, next 12 months.

OBJ-001 Suppliers are approved for payment quickly. Measure: average days from invoice receipt to approval. Baseline 14 days (ERP log, January to April). Target 5 days by 2026-12-31. Owner: Financial Controller.
OBJ-002 Suppliers are paid on agreed terms. Measure: share of invoices paid within terms. Baseline 62 percent. Target 95 percent by 2027-03-31. Owner: Financial Controller.

Alignment: both serve Finance strategy goal 2 (be a customer suppliers want) and the future state in which approval is routed by rule inside the ERP.
Priority: OBJ-002 first, as supplier holds are the most severe consequence; OBJ-001 is its main lever.
Achievability: ERP workflow capacity confirmed by the ERP lead on 22 May; budget covers configuration only.
Measurement: weekly from the ERP workflow log, reported to the finance leadership meeting.
