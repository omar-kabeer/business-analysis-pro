# Product Vision Brief: Supplier Payment Status Portal

1. Vision: suppliers never have to ask when they will be paid.
2. Target customer and job: small and mid-size suppliers of a UK manufacturer. When an invoice is outstanding, I want to know its status and expected payment date, so that I can plan cash flow without chasing. Today they phone or email AP; replies take 2 days on average and 40 percent of AP calls are status queries (call log, March to May).
3. Value proposition: self-service status and a reliable payment date, for suppliers; fewer interruptions for AP.
4. Measures: north star is the share of status queries answered by self-service (baseline 0 percent, target 60 percent in 12 months). Supporting signals: AP status calls a week (baseline 210, target 90); supplier satisfaction with payment communication (baseline 3.1 of 5, target 4.0).
5. Pillars: accurate status from the ERP, no login friction for suppliers, and AP control over what is shown.
6. Horizon: first 6 months cover status and payment date for the top 200 suppliers; later horizons consider dispute submission.
7. Not doing: supplier invoice submission, early payment financing.
Evidence: EXP-003 fake door test, 31 percent click-through. Assumption: ERP payment run dates are reliable enough to publish; to be tested with treasury in July.
