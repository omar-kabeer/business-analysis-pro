Scope: how invoices are approved today and why exceptions stall. Future-state design waits for the June workshop.
OUT-001 A confirmed as-is approval process with timings (feeds the current state assessment).
OUT-002 The top causes of exception delay, ranked by AP (feeds root cause analysis).

ACT-001 Observation of four clerks for a morning, because the procedure manual differs from practice (OUT-001).
ACT-002 Interviews with six budget holders, because they are spread across sites and short of time (OUT-001).
ACT-003 Focus group with the AP team, because clerks build on each other's examples (OUT-002).

Participants checked against the stakeholder register; clerks unavailable at month end.
