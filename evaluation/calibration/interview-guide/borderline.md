# Interview Guide

Objective: learn how budget holders decide to approve an invoice, to inform routing rules. Interviewee: a budget holder.

Questions:
1. How do you approve invoices today?
2. Is the current process too slow?
3. What problems do you have?
4. Would a mobile app help?

Notes:
- Fact: approves about 30 invoices a week on the phone.
- Opinion: emails lack context.
- Wants a mobile app.

Unknown: whether other approvers also use the phone.
