# Business Case: Invoice Approval Workflow

Summary: approvals take 14 days, so we lose about 60,000 pounds a year of discounts. We recommend the ERP workflow module.

Outcomes: approval in 5 days; 95 percent of invoices paid on time.
Options: do nothing; ERP workflow module (recommended); standalone tool (more expensive).

Costs: 150,000 pounds up front, 20,000 pounds a year to run.
Benefits: 110,000 pounds a year (discounts and AP time).
NPV at 8 percent over 3 years: about 82,000 pounds. Payback about 20 months.

Risks: approvers may not use the tool; we will train them.
Decision: approve 150,000 pounds.
