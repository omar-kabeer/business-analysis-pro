# Stakeholder Register: Supplier Invoice Approval

Scope: everyone who approves, submits, pays, audits, or supports supplier invoices in the UK entity. Sources: org chart (April), AP process walkthrough, finance steering minutes.

| ID | Stakeholder | BABOK role | Interest | Influence | Attitude | Needs and pains | Engagement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| STK-001 | Finance Director (sponsor) | Sponsor | High | High | Supportive | Close within 3 days; late-payment penalties visible to the board | Monthly steering; approves scope and budget |
| STK-002 | AP clerks (6) | End user | High | Medium | Cautious | Rekeying from PDFs; chasing approvers by email | Weekly show and tell; two join UAT |
| STK-003 | Budget-holding approvers (about 140) | End user | Medium | Medium | Resistant | Approvals interrupt other work; unclear what to check | Survey plus 5 interviews across departments |
| STK-004 | Suppliers (top 50 by volume) | Customer | High | Low | Unknown | Late and unpredictable payment | Supplier survey via procurement |
| STK-005 | Internal audit | Regulator (internal) | Medium | High | Neutral | Evidence of segregation of duties | Review of control design before build |
| STK-006 | IT service desk | Operational support | Low | Medium | Neutral | Support load at go-live | Informed monthly; training before launch |

Map: STK-001 and STK-005 manage closely; STK-002 and STK-003 keep satisfied and involved; STK-004 keep informed; STK-006 monitor.
Assumption: STK-003 resistance is inferred from 5 interviews; confirm with the survey (due 14 June).
Persona: "Busy budget holder" represents STK-003, based on 5 interviews and a survey of 42 approvers: wants to approve between meetings; unsure what to check.
