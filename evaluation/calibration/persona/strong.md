# PER-002 Dana, the Solo Finance Contact

Segment: suppliers with under 50 staff and one person on finance; about 70 percent of 1,800 active suppliers. Research: 12 supplier interviews (9 in this segment), AP call log by supplier size.

Role: office manager who also does the books (7 of 9). Context: laptop at the office, phone on site visits; checks finance twice a week.
Goal: know when money will arrive so payroll is safe (9 of 9).
Pains: no status after sending an invoice; phone callbacks take 2 days (interviews; call log).
Behaviour: would rather check a website at 7 in the morning than phone in the day; a wrong date is worse than none (6 of 9).

Design implications: no login, because Dana will not manage another account; show a date range until dates are proven.
Assumption A-101: phone use exceeds laptop for status checks; to be tested with analytics.
Set: PER-003 Raj covers large suppliers with AP teams (30 percent), who want bulk data or an API.
