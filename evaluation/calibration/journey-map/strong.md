# Customer Journey Map: Small Supplier, Invoice to Payment

Persona PER-002: sole finance contact at a 20-person supplier; goal is to know when money arrives so payroll is safe. Scope: from sending an invoice to payment arriving. Research: 12 supplier interviews (May 2026, RES-001) and 2,600 status calls in the AP log (RES-002).

Send invoice: emails PDF to the AP inbox (touchpoint: email). "Did it arrive?" Emotion 3. No acknowledgement (9 of 12, RES-001).
Wait: checks the bank daily (bank). "Is it approved yet?" Emotion 2. No status anywhere (RES-001).
Chase: phones AP (phone). "I'm wasting a morning." Emotion 1. Two-day callback; answer often "don't know" (RES-002).
Get paid: receives remittance (email). "Which invoices is this for?" Emotion 3. No invoice numbers on remittance (5 of 12).

Moment that matters: the chase call, the lowest point and the biggest cost on both sides; drop-off from self-reliance happens here.
Opportunities: OPP-001 status and payment date without login (high; to the portal PRD). OPP-002 acknowledge every invoice (medium; to the AP process owner).
Assumption A-051: large suppliers share this journey; to be tested with four interviews.
