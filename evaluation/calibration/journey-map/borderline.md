# Supplier Journey Map

Stages: send invoice, wait, chase, get paid.

| Stage | Actions | Feelings | Pain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Send invoice | Emails invoice | Neutral | No acknowledgement |
| Wait | Waits | Anxious | No status |
| Chase | Calls AP | Frustrated | Slow answers |
| Get paid | Receives money | Relieved | Remittance unclear |

Opportunities: a status portal; better remittances.
Based on 12 supplier interviews in May.
