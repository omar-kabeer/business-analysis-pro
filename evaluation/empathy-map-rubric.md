# Empathy Map Evaluation Rubric

Assess an Empathy Map against `templates/empathy-map.md`, BABOK Stakeholder List, Map, or Personas (10.43), and the Agile Perspective (11.1). The map is judged on whether it reflects evidence about a real segment in a real situation, and whether it points to design opportunities. Used by the ux skill.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Segment and context | The persona or segment and the specific situation being mapped are named, so the map is not a generic portrait. |
| 2 | Evidence basis | Content comes from research such as interviews, observation, or data, the sources are cited, and assumptions are labelled as such. |
| 3 | Says | Says captures what users actually say, ideally verbatim, and is distinct from what they think. |
| 4 | Thinks and feels | Thinks and Feels capture inferred concerns and emotions, marked as inference, and are specific to the context. |
| 5 | Does | Does records observable actions and behaviours, including workarounds, not intentions. |
| 6 | Pains and gains | Pains and gains are specific, grounded in the quadrants above, and include how the user measures success. |
| 7 | Tensions surfaced | Contradictions between what users say and what they do are called out rather than smoothed over. |
| 8 | Design opportunities | The map links to the persona and journey map and leads to named design opportunities or questions for further research. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or 20 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 14.

Record findings by severity with specific fixes. Route material issues back to the ux skill for revision. An artefact that does not pass may still be useful as a draft; its quality state must remain visible.
