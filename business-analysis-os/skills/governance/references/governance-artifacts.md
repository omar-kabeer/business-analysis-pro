# Governance Artifacts Reference

## RAID log

- Risks: uncertain events that could affect the outcome. Track cause, effect, probability, impact, owner, response, status.
- Assumptions: things taken as true. Track validity and the impact if wrong.
- Issues: problems that have already happened. Track impact, owner, action, status.
- Dependencies: reliance on another team, system, or event. Track direction, owner, due date, status.

## Risk register detail

- Score probability and impact on a consistent scale (for example 1 to 5 each); severity is their product.
- Response strategies: avoid, reduce (mitigate), transfer, accept.
- Assign a single owner and a review date. Track residual risk after the response.

## Decision log

Record: decision, date, decision-maker, options considered, rationale, and any reversal conditions. A good decision log lets a newcomer understand why, not just what.

## Requirements traceability matrix

Columns: business need, requirement ID, design or solution component, test case, and status. Maintain forward and backward links so change impact is visible and coverage gaps are obvious.

## Change control

Capture: change request, description, reason, impact on scope, cost, schedule, and risk, decision, approver, and date. Small changes still get logged; scope creep is the sum of unlogged changes.

## Release readiness checklist (example criteria)

- All must-have requirements met and tested.
- Acceptance criteria satisfied; no open critical or high defects.
- Non-functional needs verified (performance, security, accessibility).
- Documentation, training, and support in place.
- Rollback plan defined; stakeholders signed off.
Record the outcome as go, no-go, or conditional-go with the outstanding items and owners.
