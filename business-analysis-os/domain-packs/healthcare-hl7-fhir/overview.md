# Healthcare and HL7 FHIR Pack

Knowledge and deliverables for business analysis on healthcare initiatives, centred on health data interoperability with HL7 FHIR and the privacy and security rules that govern health data. Load this pack when an initiative involves clinical or health data, electronic health records, health data exchange, or healthcare regulation.

## When to load

Any mention of HL7, FHIR, HL7 v2, electronic health records, EHR or EMR, patient data, clinical data exchange, US Core, USCDI, TEFCA, HIPAA, or a healthcare product or integration.

## What it covers

- `terminology.md`: HL7 v2 and FHIR, the FHIR resource model, US Core and USCDI, profiles, and SMART on FHIR.
- `regulation.md`: the privacy, security, and interoperability landscape, with dates and a note to verify current status.
- `deliverables/`: healthcare-specific templates (FHIR resource mapping, patient consent and access requirements, and a healthcare compliance checklist).

## How it plugs into the OS

- The business-analysis, requirements, and data-analysis skills use the terminology and deliverables for health data requirements.
- The architecture skill draws on FHIR resources and APIs for integration.
- The governance and quality skills use the compliance checklist as a control.

## A note on accuracy

Healthcare standards and regulations change on fixed timelines and vary by jurisdiction (this pack leans on the United States framework). The dates in `regulation.md` were current as of mid-2026 and are cited. Verify the current position with the standards body or regulator before relying on it.
