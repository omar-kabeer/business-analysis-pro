# Definition of Ready

## Purpose

A shared, lightweight gate that a backlog item must pass before it enters a sprint. It prevents the team from starting work that is ambiguous, unsized, or blocked. Tailor the list to your team; keep it short enough that it is actually used.

## Checklist

Mark each item Yes, No, or Not applicable.

- [ ] The story follows the standard form (As a role, I want capability, so that benefit) and is understood by the team.
- [ ] It is INVEST-compliant (independent, negotiable, valuable, estimable, small, testable).
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are written and cover the happy path and key edge cases.
- [ ] It is small enough to complete within one sprint.
- [ ] The team has sized or estimated it.
- [ ] Dependencies are identified and none are blocking.
- [ ] Required designs, data, or inputs are available or linked.
- [ ] Non-functional needs relevant to the story are noted.
- [ ] It traces to an epic or outcome.
- [ ] There are no open questions that would stop work starting.

## Outcome

A story that meets every applicable item is Ready. A story that fails one or more is sent back to refinement with the specific gaps noted, not pulled into the sprint.
