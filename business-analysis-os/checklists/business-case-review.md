# Business Case Review

## Purpose

Review a business case before it goes for a funding decision, so the recommendation rests on sound need, honest numbers, and acknowledged risk. Based on BABOK Business Cases (10.7) and Financial Analysis (10.20).

## Need and outcomes

- [ ] The business need is stated from the enterprise perspective and linked to strategy.
- [ ] The problem or opportunity is quantified, including the cost of doing nothing.
- [ ] Desired outcomes are measurable and independent of any specific solution.

## Options

- [ ] Real alternatives are assessed, including the do-nothing option.
- [ ] Each option is assessed on scope, feasibility, and its assumptions, risks, and constraints.

## Financials

- [ ] Every cost and benefit has a stated assumption and source.
- [ ] The time horizon is long enough for the solution to be in full use.
- [ ] NPV, IRR, payback, ROI, and total cost of ownership are computed and interpreted correctly.
- [ ] IRR is compared to the hurdle rate; NPV is the primary decision metric.
- [ ] Sensitivity or scenario analysis is present, with a break-even point for the key driver.
- [ ] Non-financial value and intangibles are recorded and tied to strategy.

## Recommendation and risk

- [ ] The recommendation follows from the analysis, not from preference.
- [ ] Key risks are identified with responses and owners.
- [ ] The decision required and the approver are explicit.
- [ ] The numbers are consistent across the document and any visuals.
