# Customer Journey Map

## Purpose

Map a user's experience across the stages of a task or relationship, exposing where it breaks and where the opportunities are. Grounded in the UX artifacts reference and BABOK Stakeholder List, Map, or Personas (10.43). Use a current-state map to understand and a future-state map to design.

## Persona summary

State who this journey is for: their goal, context, and the top pains that matter for this journey.

## Journey stages

For each stage, capture what the user does, thinks, and feels, the pain points, and the opportunities.

| Stage | User actions | Thoughts | Emotions (high or low) | Pain points | Opportunities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |  |  |  |

## Moments that matter

Call out the few moments that most shape the overall experience (first impression, a decision point, a failure and recovery), and what good looks like at each.

## Opportunities and next steps

Summarise the prioritised opportunities and where they hand off (to requirements, product, or process).

## Review criteria

- Stages cover the journey end to end from the user's perspective.
- Emotions and pain points are based on evidence, with assumptions labelled.
- The map surfaces specific, prioritised opportunities.
- It hands off cleanly to requirements or product.
