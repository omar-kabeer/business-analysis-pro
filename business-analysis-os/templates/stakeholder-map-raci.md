# Stakeholder Map and RACI Matrix

## Purpose

Turn the stakeholder register into two decision tools: a stakeholder map that positions each stakeholder by influence and interest so engagement effort is spent well, and a responsibility matrix (RACI) that makes clear who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for each activity or decision. These are the Stakeholder Map (BABOK 10.43) and the RACI form of the Roles and Permissions Matrix (BABOK 10.39).

## Inputs

The stakeholder register (roles, interest, influence), the solution scope, and the list of key activities or decisions for the initiative.

## Part A: Stakeholder map (influence against interest)

Position each stakeholder in the matrix below by their level of influence (authority over the change) and their interest in it. The quadrant sets the engagement strategy. An onion diagram is an alternative view when you want to show how close each stakeholder sits to the solution.

| Quadrant | Influence | Interest | Engagement strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manage closely | High | High | Involve in decisions, co-design, frequent two-way contact |
| Keep satisfied | High | Low | Consult on direction, keep confident, avoid surprises |
| Keep informed | Low | High | Update regularly, use as a source of detail and feedback |
| Monitor | Low | Low | Light-touch updates, watch for change in stance |

Placement:

| Stakeholder | Influence | Interest | Quadrant | Engagement strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | High / Med / Low | High / Med / Low |  |  |

## Part B: RACI matrix

List the initiative's activities or decisions down the rows and the stakeholders or roles across the columns. In each cell record R, A, C, or I.

- R (Responsible): does the work.
- A (Accountable): answerable for the outcome and the final decision. Exactly one A per row.
- C (Consulted): gives input before the work is done (two-way).
- I (Informed): told after the fact (one-way).

| Activity or decision | Stakeholder 1 | Stakeholder 2 | Stakeholder 3 | Stakeholder 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example: approve requirements | I | A | C | R |
|  |  |  |  |  |

Rules to check: every row has exactly one A, at least one R, and no stakeholder is overloaded with A across the whole matrix.

## Assumptions

Note anything assumed about authority or decision rights and confirm it with the accountable stakeholders.

## Review criteria

- Every stakeholder from the register appears in the map.
- Each map quadrant has an engagement strategy that matches the stakeholder's influence and interest.
- Each RACI row has exactly one Accountable and at least one Responsible.
- Consulted and Informed are used deliberately, not by default.
