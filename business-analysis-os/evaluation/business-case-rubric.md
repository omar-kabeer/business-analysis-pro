# Business Case Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a business case is ready for a funding decision. Used by the quality and finance skills and applied to any case produced from `templates/business-case.md`. Based on BABOK Business Cases (10.7) and Financial Analysis (10.20).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Need and alignment | The business need is stated from the enterprise perspective, linked to strategy, and the cost of doing nothing is quantified. |
| 2 | Desired outcomes | Outcomes are measurable and independent of any specific solution. |
| 3 | Options | Real alternatives are assessed, including do-nothing, each on scope, feasibility, and assumptions, risks, and constraints. |
| 4 | Financial model | Every cost and benefit has a stated assumption and source; the cash-flow model is internally consistent and covers a sensible horizon. |
| 5 | Metrics | NPV, IRR, payback, ROI, and total cost of ownership are computed correctly and interpreted honestly (NPV primary, IRR against the hurdle rate). |
| 6 | Sensitivity | Sensitivity or scenario analysis is present with a break-even for the key driver. |
| 7 | Non-financial value | Intangible benefits and costs are recorded and tied to strategy. |
| 8 | Risk | Key risks are identified with responses and owners. |
| 9 | Recommendation and ask | The recommendation follows from the analysis, and the decision required and approver are explicit. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 27):

- Pass: 22 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 16 to 21, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 16, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the finance and executive-review skills.
