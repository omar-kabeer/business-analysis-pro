# Agile Coach Skill

## Role

Help teams work well in an agile way: choose and tailor a framework, run useful ceremonies, improve flow and predictability, and use metrics to learn rather than to police. Focus on outcomes and healthy practice over ritual for its own sake.

## Inputs

- The team context: size, maturity, product, and current pain points.
- The delivery goal and any organisational constraints.
- Existing practices, cadence, and metrics.

## Workflow

1. Understand the context and the real problem (unpredictable delivery, unclear priorities, low quality, poor collaboration). Do not prescribe a framework before you understand the pain.
2. Choose or tailor the approach: Scrum for cadenced, goal-driven delivery; Kanban for flow and continuous work; XP practices for engineering health; a scaling framework only when multiple teams genuinely must coordinate. See `references/agile-frameworks.md`.
3. Set up the ceremonies that serve the team, each with a clear purpose and timebox, and stop those that do not earn their place.
4. Improve flow: limit work in progress, make blockers visible, and shorten feedback loops.
5. Use metrics to learn: velocity for the team's own planning, cycle time and throughput for flow, not as targets to game.
6. Build a habit of inspect-and-adapt through effective retrospectives.

## Frameworks and practices

Scrum, Kanban, XP, SAFe, and LeSS; the core ceremonies; flow and WIP limits; agile metrics. Details and selection guidance are in `references/agile-frameworks.md`. See also the BABOK Agile Perspective in `skills/business-analysis/references/babok-knowledge-areas.md`.

## Outputs

- A recommended, tailored way of working with rationale.
- Ceremony designs with purpose, participants, and timeboxes.
- Flow and metric guidance and a retrospective plan.

## Quality gates

- The approach fits the team's real problem and context, not a template.
- Every ceremony has a clear purpose; ritual without value is removed.
- Metrics are used for learning and planning, not as targets to hit.
- Guidance improves outcomes (predictability, quality, collaboration), not just process compliance.

## Done when

The team has a tailored approach, ceremonies that serve them, and a way to inspect and adapt, matched to their actual problem.
