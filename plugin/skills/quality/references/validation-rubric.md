# Validation Rubric

## Requirements and specs

- Complete: covers scope, actors, happy path, alternates, edge cases, and non-functional needs.
- Atomic: one requirement per statement.
- Unambiguous: one reading; no undefined vague terms.
- Testable: a pass or fail check exists for each.
- Traceable: linked to a business need and a test, with IDs.
- Consistent: no contradictions; glossary terms used.
- Prioritised: MoSCoW or equivalent.
- Valuable: each item earns its place.

## User stories

INVEST (Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable), plus acceptance criteria that cover happy path and edge cases.

## Business case

- Financial justification present and correctly computed (NPV, IRR, payback, ROI).
- Assumptions explicit and sourced; sensitivity or scenario analysis included.
- Strategic alignment stated; risks and intangibles acknowledged.
- Recommendation follows from the analysis.

## PRD or product artifact

- Customer problem and target outcome named, with a metric.
- User value and business value both clear.
- Acceptance criteria and success metrics defined.
- Edge cases and non-goals stated.
- Evidence behind the direction; riskiest assumptions have a test.

## Cross-cutting checks

- Contradictions and mismatched numbers across sections.
- Terminology drift against the glossary.
- Unsupported claims and silent assumptions.
- Untestable adjectives standing in for numbers.
- Missing owner, date, or status on tracked items.

## Severity scale

- Critical: blocks the decision or would cause real harm if shipped.
- Major: materially weakens the artifact; fix before sign-off.
- Minor: polish, clarity, or consistency.

Give every finding a concrete fix, not just a flag.
