# Stakeholder Engagement Approach

## Purpose

Plan how the team collaborates and communicates with stakeholders across the initiative, and capture the stakeholder analysis that informs it. This is the working form of the BABOK Plan Stakeholder Engagement task (3.2) and its output, the Stakeholder Engagement Approach. Cover every stakeholder the change affects, and match the engagement to each.

## 3. Collaboration approach

Describe how the team collaborates with each stakeholder or group, matched to their influence and interest.

## 5. Needs and expectations

Capture what each stakeholder needs from the effort and what they expect of it.

## 6. Cadence and triggers

State the regular cadence of engagement and the events that trigger additional engagement.
