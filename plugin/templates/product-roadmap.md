# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

Communicate product direction as outcomes over time, not a dated list of features. A now-next-later roadmap sets expectations about what the team is working toward and how confident it is, and adapts as discovery changes the evidence. Use it to align stakeholders without over-committing to dates.

## Principles

- Lead with outcomes and problems, not solutions.
- Communicate confidence, not false date precision. Near-term items are more certain than later ones.
- Revisit the roadmap when discovery, data, or priorities change.

## Not on the roadmap

State explicitly what the team has decided not to do for now, and why. This prevents recurring questions and manages expectations.

## Review cadence

State how often the roadmap is reviewed and updated, and who is involved.

## Review criteria

- Each item names the outcome and problem it serves, not just a feature.
- Confidence is communicated and later items are held loosely.
- Themes give the roadmap a coherent narrative.
- Explicit non-goals are recorded.
