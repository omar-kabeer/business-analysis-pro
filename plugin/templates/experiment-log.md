# Experiment and Post-Mortem Log

## Purpose

Record the discovery experiments and launch post-mortems that turn activity into learning: the hypothesis, the risk being tested, the method, the result, and the decision. A good log stops the team relearning the same lessons and keeps discovery honest.

## Launch post-mortem

For a completed launch, review what happened against what was expected.

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |

## Review criteria

- Each experiment states the hypothesis and threshold before the result.
- The risk type and method are recorded.
- The decision (persevere, pivot, drop) is explicit.
- Post-mortems reach a root cause and a concrete change, not blame.
