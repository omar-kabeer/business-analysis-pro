# Workshop Plan

## Purpose

Plan and run a facilitated workshop that brings stakeholders together to elicit, refine, or decide, then confirm the results. This is the workshop form of the BABOK Elicitation Activity Plan (Prepare for Elicitation, 4.1), the Workshops technique (10.50), and Conduct and Confirm Elicitation Results (4.2 and 4.3).

## Preparation

State the desired outcomes up front so success is clear. Select participants from the stakeholder register to cover the needed knowledge and decision authority. Prepare pre-work and materials (current-state models, data, templates). Confirm logistics and send the agenda and any pre-read in advance.

## Agenda

| Time | Item | Technique or activity | Facilitator | Intended output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | Welcome, purpose, desired outcomes, ground rules |  |  | Shared understanding of the goal |

|  | Divergent activity | Brainstorming, affinity grouping |  | Options and ideas |
|  | Convergent activity | Prioritisation, decision analysis |  | Decisions |
|  | Assumptions, risks, open questions | Parking lot |  | Captured items with owners |
|  | Recap, decisions, actions, next steps |  |  | Agreed actions and owners |

Timebox each item and keep a visible parking lot for issues that would derail the agenda.

## Ground rules

Agree a short set of ground rules (one conversation at a time, everyone contributes, decisions by the agreed method, park tangents). This keeps a mixed group productive.

## After the workshop (confirm)

Distribute the notes, confirm decisions and actions with participants (Confirm Elicitation Results), and feed the outputs into analysis or requirements.

## Review criteria

- Desired outcomes were stated up front and were met or explicitly carried over.
- Participants covered the knowledge and decision authority the outcomes needed.
- Every agenda item had a timebox, a technique, and an intended output.
- Decisions, actions, and parked items were captured with owners and confirmed afterward.

## House style

Write the plan and the notes in the house style: clear, direct, and with no em dashes. See `docs/methodology/editorial-style.md`.
