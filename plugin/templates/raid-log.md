# RAID Log

## Purpose

Track the four things that most often derail delivery in one place: Risks, Assumptions, Issues, and Dependencies. The RAID log keeps each item visible, owned, and current, so nothing important is lost. It draws on BABOK Item Tracking (10.26) and Risk Analysis and Management (10.38).

## Review criteria

- Every item has an owner, a date, and a status.
- Risks describe cause, event, and effect, not just a label.
- Assumptions carry an impact-if-wrong and a validation status.
- The log is reviewed on a stated cadence and closed items are marked, not deleted.
