# User Acceptance Test Plan

## Purpose

Define how the business will prove that the delivered solution meets the agreed requirements, and set the conditions under which it will be accepted.

## Scope

In scope: requirements, processes, and interfaces being tested.
Out of scope: what is deliberately not tested, and why.
Assumptions and dependencies: environments, data, people, and upstream deliveries.

## Approach

Describe the testing approach: scenario-based end-to-end business tasks, supported by rule-level cases for boundaries and exceptions, plus time-boxed exploratory sessions.

## Entry criteria

Requirements baselined; build deployed and smoke-tested; environment stable; data loaded; cases reviewed and approved; testers trained and available; defect process agreed.

## Exit criteria

Planned coverage executed; zero open critical; zero open high or a written accepted exception per defect; all mediums triaged with owners and dates; non-functional requirements verified; sign-off by the named business owner.
