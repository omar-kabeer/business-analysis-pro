# Business Rules Catalogue

## Purpose

Capture the rules that govern decisions and behaviour, separately from the requirements that enforce them, so a rule can change in one place without rewriting every requirement. Based on Business Rules Analysis.

## Rule types

- Constraint: something that must or must not happen.
- Computation: how a value is calculated.
- Inference: a conclusion drawn when conditions hold.
- Guideline: a recommended, non-mandatory practice.

## Review criteria

- Each rule is atomic and stated in business language.
- Rules are separated from the requirements that enforce them.
- Each rule names its source and owner.
- Rules do not contradict one another.
