# Elicitation Activity Plan Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether an elicitation activity plan is ready to run. The plan defines the desired outcomes, techniques, participants, and logistics for elicitation. Used by the elicitation and quality skills and applied to any plan produced from `templates/elicitation-activity-plan.md`. Based on BABOK Prepare for Elicitation (4.1).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Desired outcomes | The outcomes and the work products the elicitation must produce are defined. |
| 2 | Techniques | The techniques selected suit those outcomes and the stakeholders involved. |
| 3 | Stakeholders | The right participants are identified and their availability is considered. |
| 4 | Scope | The scope and purpose of each activity are clear. |
| 5 | Logistics | Schedule, location, and resources are arranged. |
| 6 | Supporting materials | The materials needed are identified and procured. |
| 7 | Collaboration | The conditions that foster collaboration during the activity are considered. |
| 8 | Fit | The plan is consistent with the business analysis approach and the stakeholder engagement approach. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 14, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
