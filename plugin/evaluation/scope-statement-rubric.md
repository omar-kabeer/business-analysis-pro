# Scope Statement Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a scope statement defines the boundary of the change unambiguously. A scope statement defines what is in and out of scope for the change. Used by the business-analysis and ba-planning skills. Based on BABOK scope and solution-scope practice.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | In scope explicit | What is in scope is stated explicitly. |
| 2 | Out of scope explicit | What is out of scope is stated, to remove ambiguity. |
| 3 | Boundaries | The boundaries are drawn so the work cannot sprawl. |
| 4 | Deliverables | The deliverables the scope covers are identified. |
| 5 | Assumptions and constraints | Assumptions and constraints bearing on scope are stated. |
| 6 | Agreed | The scope statement is agreed with the relevant stakeholders. |
| 7 | Unambiguous | A reader cannot reasonably misread what is in or out. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the ba-planning skill.
