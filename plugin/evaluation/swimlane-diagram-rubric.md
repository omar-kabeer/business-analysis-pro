# Swimlane Diagram Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a swimlane diagram assigns process steps to the right roles with clear handoffs. A swimlane diagram arranges process steps in lanes, one per role or actor. Used by the process-modelling and visual-modelling skills. Based on BABOK Process Modelling (10.35).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Lanes are roles | Each lane represents a role or actor, and every role in the process has a lane. |
| 2 | Steps placed correctly | Each step sits in the lane of the role that performs it. |
| 3 | Handoffs clear | Handoffs between lanes are shown explicitly. |
| 4 | Start and end | The process has a clear start and end. |
| 5 | No orphan steps | Every step belongs to a lane and connects into the flow. |
| 6 | Decisions complete | Decision points have complete, exclusive branches. |
| 7 | Readable | The diagram is legible and labelled so a reader can follow it unaided. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the process-modelling skill.
