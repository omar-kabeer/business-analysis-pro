# Requirements Management Plan Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging a requirements management plan. The plan describes the approach, traceability, and change control for managing requirements on an initiative. It is a BABOK v2 artefact; in BABOK v3 this is covered by the governance and information management approaches, so prefer those for new work. Used by the requirements and governance skills.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Approach | The approach to managing requirements is defined. |
| 2 | Traceability | How traceability is captured and maintained is defined. |
| 3 | Change control | How requirements changes are requested, assessed, and approved is defined. |
| 4 | Roles | The roles and responsibilities for requirements management are clear. |
| 5 | Tools | The tools and repository used are stated. |
| 6 | Attributes and status | How requirement attributes and status are managed is defined. |
| 7 | Currency | Where BABOK v3 approaches exist, the plan defers to them rather than duplicating. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the requirements skill.
