# Information Map Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether an information map represents the enterprise's key information soundly. An information map is a taxonomy of the key business concepts and information an enterprise uses. Used by the business-architecture and information-management skills. Based on BABOK Business Architecture Perspective (11.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Concepts | The key business information concepts are identified. |
| 2 | Taxonomy | The concepts are organized into a coherent taxonomy. |
| 3 | MECE | The categories are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive. |
| 4 | Relationships | The relationships between information concepts are shown. |
| 5 | Business language | The map uses business terms, consistent with the concept model and glossary. |
| 6 | Aligned | The map aligns to capabilities and value. |
| 7 | Readable | The map is legible and can be used unaided. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the business-architecture skill.
