# Peer Review Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a peer review was conducted soundly. A peer review is a review of a work product by peers to find defects and improve quality. Used by the quality skill. Based on BABOK Reviews (10.37).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reviewers | The reviewers have the knowledge to judge the work product. |
| 2 | Coverage | The work product was reviewed in full against a standard or checklist. |
| 3 | Defects logged | Defects and observations are logged and classified. |
| 4 | Constructive | The feedback is specific and constructive, aimed at the work not the author. |
| 5 | Actions | Actions to resolve findings are assigned and owned. |
| 6 | Exit criteria | The criteria for completing the review are defined and met. |
| 7 | Record | The review and its outcome are recorded. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the quality skill.
