# Benchmarking Study / Market Analysis Report Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a benchmarking or market analysis report is sound and actionable. The report compares the organization against benchmarks or analyses a market to inform a decision. Used by the market-research and strategy skills. Based on BABOK Benchmarking and Market Analysis (10.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Comparators valid | The benchmarks or market segments compared are relevant and defensible. |
| 2 | Method | The method of comparison is stated and applied consistently. |
| 3 | Sourced | The data is sourced, current, and credible. |
| 4 | Objective | The analysis is balanced, not slanted to a preferred conclusion. |
| 5 | Insights | The report draws insights, not just data. |
| 6 | Relevance | The findings bear on the decision the report supports. |
| 7 | Actionable | The report makes actionable recommendations. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the market-research skill.
