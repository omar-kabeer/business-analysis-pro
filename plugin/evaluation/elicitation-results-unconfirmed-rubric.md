# Elicitation Results (unconfirmed) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether unconfirmed elicitation results are captured well enough to take into confirmation. Unconfirmed elicitation results are the information captured during an elicitation activity, not yet reviewed for accuracy. Used by the elicitation and quality skills and applied to the raw output of an elicitation activity. Based on BABOK Conduct Elicitation (4.2).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Captured accurately | The information is recorded faithfully to what the activity produced. |
| 2 | Format fit | The results are captured in a format suited to the activity and usable downstream. |
| 3 | Coverage | The planned scope of the activity is covered. |
| 4 | Source noted | The source of each item is recorded. |
| 5 | Traceable to need | The items relate to the need and scope that drove the activity. |
| 6 | Session completeness | There are no obvious gaps against the elicitation plan. |
| 7 | Ready to confirm | The results are organized enough to be confirmed. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
