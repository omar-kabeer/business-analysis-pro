# Status Report Evaluation Rubric

Assess a Status Report against `templates/status-report.md` and BABOK Communicate Business Analysis Information (4.4). A status report is judged on honesty and on whether it lets the audience act; a green rating that hides a real problem fails regardless of how well the rest is written. Used by the communication and governance skills.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Audience and period | The audience, reporting period, and author are stated, and the content is pitched at what that audience needs to know or decide. |
| 2 | Honest rating | The overall rating is supported by the evidence in the report, and the reason for it is stated in plain terms. |
| 3 | Progress against plan | Scope, schedule, and budget are each shown as planned against actual, with the variance and its cause explained. |
| 4 | Risks and issues | The risks and issues shown are the ones that matter now, each specific, owned, and with an action, and they agree with the RAID log. |
| 5 | Decisions needed | Each decision is stated as a clear ask, naming who must decide and by when, and what happens if it is not made. |
| 6 | Forward view | The next period's milestones and activities are stated, so the reader knows what to expect before the next report. |
| 7 | Consistency | Figures, dates, and statuses agree with the plan, the RAID log, and the previous report, and changes since the previous report are visible. |
| 8 | Brevity and lead | The report leads with the rating and the asks, and cuts background that does not change what the reader would do. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or 20 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 14.

Record findings by severity with specific fixes. Route material issues back to the communication skill for revision. An artefact that does not pass may still be useful as a draft; its quality state must remain visible.
