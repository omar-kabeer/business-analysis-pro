# Identified Risks Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a set of identified risks is well-formed enough to manage. Identified risks are the risks to achieving the future state or the value of a change, captured for assessment and response. Used by the risk-analysis and quality skills. Based on BABOK Assess Risks (6.3). For the fuller assessment, see the risk analysis results rubric.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Risks well-formed | Each risk states its condition and its effect on the value of the change. |
| 2 | Coverage | The main sources of risk are covered, not just the obvious ones. |
| 3 | Likelihood and impact | Each risk is assessed for likelihood and impact. |
| 4 | Owned | Each risk has an owner. |
| 5 | Response | Each risk has a response strategy. |
| 6 | Evidence | The risks are based on evidence, expert judgment, or history. |
| 7 | Traceable | The risks tie to the business objectives and the change strategy. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the risk-analysis skill.
