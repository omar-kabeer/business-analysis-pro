# Glossary of Business Domain Terms Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a domain glossary defines terms clearly and consistently. A glossary records the definitions of the business-domain terms used across the work. Used by the business-analysis and technical-writer skills. Based on BABOK Glossary (10.23).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Terms defined | Each term has a clear definition. |
| 2 | Consistent | The definitions are used consistently across the artefacts. |
| 3 | No duplicates | There are no duplicate or conflicting definitions. |
| 4 | Business language | The definitions use business language, not circular or system jargon. |
| 5 | Coverage | The terms the work relies on are all included. |
| 6 | Synonyms and acronyms | Synonyms and acronyms are cross-referenced. |
| 7 | Owned | The glossary has an owner and is kept current. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the business-analysis skill.
