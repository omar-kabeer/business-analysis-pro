# Solution Performance Analysis Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a solution performance analysis draws sound conclusions from the measures. The analysis compares the performance measurements collected against the goals and recommends how to close gaps and leverage opportunities. Used by the solution-evaluation and business-intelligence skills and applied to any analysis produced from `templates/solution-performance-analysis.md`. Based on BABOK Analyze Performance Measures (8.2).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Against goals | The analysis compares actual performance to the goals and expected value. |
| 2 | Sound method | The analytical method is appropriate and applied correctly. |
| 3 | Drivers identified | The drivers of the performance, and the root causes of any variance, are identified. |
| 4 | Conclusions supported | The conclusions follow from the data, not from assumption. |
| 5 | Uncertainty | The uncertainty and limitations of the data are acknowledged. |
| 6 | Recommendations | The analysis recommends how to solve performance gaps and leverage opportunities. |
| 7 | Traceable | The analysis ties back to the business objectives and the performance measures. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the solution-evaluation skill.
