# Process Architecture Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a process architecture organizes the enterprise's processes coherently. A process architecture shows the levels and relationships of processes across the organization. Used by the process-modelling and business-architecture skills. Based on BABOK Business Process Management Perspective (11.5).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Levels | The process levels, from value streams down to procedures, are defined. |
| 2 | Coverage | The processes of the organization in scope are covered. |
| 3 | Relationships | The relationships and handoffs between processes are shown. |
| 4 | Consistent classification | Processes are classified consistently, for example core, supporting, management. |
| 5 | No gaps or overlaps | There are no missing processes or unjustified overlaps. |
| 6 | Aligned | The architecture aligns to capabilities and value streams. |
| 7 | Readable | The architecture is legible and can be navigated unaided. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the process-modelling skill.
