# Experiment Log: Supplier Payment Status Portal

| ID | Date | Hypothesis | Risk tested | Method | Measure and success threshold (set before running) | Result | Decision | Stage and link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EXP-003 | 2 to 16 June | We believe suppliers who can check payment status themselves will call AP less, because 40 percent of AP calls ask "when will I be paid". | Value | Fake door: "Check payment status" link in the remittance email to 400 suppliers | At least 25 percent of recipients click within 14 days; below 10 percent means stop | 31 percent clicked (124 of 400); calls from clicking suppliers fell 18 percent against non-clickers over the period | Proceed to a concierge test (EXP-004) | Discovery; OPP-002 |

Learning: interest is strong, but the call reduction may reflect supplier size, because clickers skewed to larger suppliers. EXP-004 will stratify by supplier size.
