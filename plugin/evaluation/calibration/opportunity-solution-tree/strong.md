# Opportunity Solution Tree: Supplier Status

Outcome: share of status queries answered by self-service, 0 to 60 percent by June 2027.

OPP-001 "I don't know whether my invoice arrived" (9 of 12 interviews; high).
OPP-002 "I can't plan cash because I don't know when I'll be paid" (10 of 12; AP call log; high).
OPP-003 "I can't match payments to invoices" (5 of 12; medium).

SOL-001 Status lookup without login, for OPP-001. Assumes suppliers will use it (value) and the ERP gives real-time status (feasibility).
SOL-002 Expected payment date on the lookup page, for OPP-002. Assumes dates are accurate enough (feasibility).
SOL-003 Weekly payment forecast email, for OPP-002. Assumes suppliers read it (value).

EXP-003 Fake door link in remittance emails (tests SOL-001 value): 31 percent click-through; kept.
EXP-004 Compare 8 weeks of forecast and actual dates (SOL-002 feasibility): 91 percent within 1 day; kept, shown as a range.
EXP-005 Forecast email pilot (SOL-003 value): 8 percent open rate; SOL-003 parked on 19 June.
