# Current State Assessment

Business need: invoice approval is slow, so 38 percent of invoices are paid late and suppliers complain.

Scope: accounts payable.

Processes: invoices arrive by email, clerks match them in the ERP, then email the budget holder for approval. Exceptions are chased by phone. Approval takes 14 days on average (ERP workflow log, January to April).
Culture: approvers are busy and do not prioritise approvals.
Systems: the ERP; no workflow tool.
Policies: invoices must match the PO within tolerance.
External: suppliers expect faster payment.

Summary: the current process relies on email and is slow.
