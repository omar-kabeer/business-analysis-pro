# Product Vision Brief: Supplier Portal

Vision: suppliers always know when they will be paid.
Customer: suppliers who phone or email AP to ask about payment status.
Value: self-service status for suppliers and fewer calls for AP.
Measures: AP status calls a week, from about 200 to under 100. Supplier satisfaction should go up.
Pillars: status tracking, invoice submission, dispute management, early payment, analytics.
Horizon: all features within 6 months.
