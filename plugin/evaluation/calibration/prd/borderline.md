# PRD: Supplier Portal

Problem: suppliers call AP a lot to ask about payments.
Users: small supplier finance contacts who need to plan cash.
Goal: reduce calls to AP.
Scope: status lookup and payment dates in release 1.

Requirements:
- Suppliers can look up invoice status by invoice number. Given a valid number, status shows; given an unknown one, "not found" shows.
- Suppliers can see when they will be paid.
- The portal should be fast and secure.

Depends on the ERP API. Launch to all suppliers in September.
