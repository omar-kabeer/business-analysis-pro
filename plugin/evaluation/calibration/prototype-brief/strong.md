# Prototype Brief: Approver Review Screen

Question: can a first-time approver find what to check and approve an invoice within 3 minutes without help? Decision that follows: whether the single-screen layout goes into build or we test the two-step layout instead. Answered when 8 of 10 participants complete the task unaided.

Type and fidelity: throw-away, clickable mock-up. Fidelity is right because layout and wording are the uncertainty; the data and back end are not.

Scope: included, invoice summary, match status, line detail, approve and reject with reason. Stubbed, the PDF viewer. Absent, delegation and bulk approval.

Traces to: STORY-018, STORY-019, NFR-005.

Session plan: 10 approvers from 4 departments, none of whom used the pilot; three tasks (approve a clean invoice, reject a mismatched one, find the PO number); facilitator from UX; recorded with consent.

Findings (sessions 18 to 20 June): 7 of 10 completed unaided within 3 minutes. Six missed the match status badge; it moves above the fold in the next iteration.
