# Business Analysis Package Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a business analysis package is ready to communicate to its audience. A business analysis package is a document, presentation, or other collection of text, matrices, diagrams, and models that represents business analysis information for a stakeholder group. Used by the technical-writer and quality skills. Based on BABOK Communicate Business Analysis Information (4.4) and the Glossary.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Purpose clear | Why the package exists, such as a review, an approval, an input to design, or reuse, is stated. |
| 2 | Audience fit | The content and format suit what the intended audience understands and needs. |
| 3 | Completeness | The package contains the business analysis information the purpose requires. |
| 4 | Correct forms | It uses appropriate forms, whether text, matrices, diagrams, or models, for the content. |
| 5 | Clarity | It conveys the information clearly and in a usable form. |
| 6 | Traceable | The items in the package trace to their source. |
| 7 | Obligations | Any regulatory or contractual constraints on the package are met. |
| 8 | Consistency | The package is internally consistent, with no conflicting content. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 14, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the technical-writer skill.
