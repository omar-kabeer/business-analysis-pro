# Request for Proposal (RFP) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a request for proposal will elicit comparable, evaluable proposals. An RFP is a requirements document issued when an organization seeks a formal proposal from vendors. Used by the procurement-contracts and vendor-evaluation skills. Based on BABOK procurement and solicitation practice.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Requirements complete | The requirements the solution must meet are complete and clear. |
| 2 | Evaluation criteria | The criteria that proposals will be evaluated against are stated. |
| 3 | Response format | The required proposal structure and format are defined so responses can be compared. |
| 4 | Scope and context | The scope, background, and constraints are given. |
| 5 | Commercial terms | Pricing, contractual, and service expectations are stated. |
| 6 | Process and timeline | The submission process, deadline, and decision timeline are clear. |
| 7 | Terms and conditions | Legal and procurement terms are included. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the procurement-contracts skill.
