# Horizontal Prototype Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a horizontal prototype covers the intended breadth. A horizontal prototype shows broad coverage of the interface or capability at shallow depth. Used by the prototyping and ux skills. Based on BABOK Prototyping (10.36).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Breadth | The prototype covers the range of screens or capabilities it is meant to show. |
| 2 | Shallow by design | The depth is deliberately shallow, matching the prototype's purpose. |
| 3 | Navigation | The breadth is connected so a reviewer can move across it. |
| 4 | Requirements traceable | The coverage traces to the requirements it represents. |
| 5 | Testable | It is concrete enough to review with users for breadth and flow. |
| 6 | Scope clear | It is clear that depth is not represented, to avoid false expectations. |
| 7 | Feedback captured | Feedback on coverage and flow is captured. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the prototyping skill.
