# Request for Information (RFI) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a request for information will gather comparable, useful supplier information. An RFI is a formal method to collect information from potential suppliers. Used by the procurement-contracts and vendor-evaluation skills. Based on BABOK procurement and solicitation practice.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Information needs clear | The information sought is stated clearly and is relevant to the decision it supports. |
| 2 | Structured | Questions are structured so responses can be compared across suppliers. |
| 3 | Scope | The context and purpose are given so suppliers can respond appropriately. |
| 4 | Response format | The required response format and any constraints are stated. |
| 5 | Timeline | The submission deadline and process are clear. |
| 6 | Proportionate | The request asks only for what is needed at this stage. |
| 7 | Contact and terms | The point of contact and any terms of use for the information are stated. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the procurement-contracts skill.
