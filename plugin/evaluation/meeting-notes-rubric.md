# Meeting Notes Evaluation Rubric

Assess Meeting Notes against `templates/meeting-notes.md`, BABOK Confirm Elicitation Results (4.3), and Communicate Business Analysis Information (4.4). Notes are judged as a record of what was decided and who owns what next; only what participants confirmed counts as agreed. Used by the communication and elicitation skills.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent or misleading, 1 weak, 2 adequate for the stated stage, 3 strong and well supported.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Context | The meeting, date, facilitator, note taker, and attendance are recorded, so the authority of what was decided can be judged. |
| 2 | Purpose and agenda | The purpose and the agenda items covered are stated, and items that were not reached are noted. |
| 3 | Decisions | Each decision is stated as a clear outcome with its rationale and owner, and discussion without a decision is not recorded as one. |
| 4 | Actions | Each action is specific, has a single owner, and has a due date. |
| 5 | Open items | Unresolved questions are parked with an owner and a next step rather than lost. |
| 6 | Accuracy and attribution | Statements are attributed where attribution matters, and the notes record what was said, not the note taker's interpretation. |
| 7 | Confirmation and circulation | The notes were circulated promptly and confirmed by participants, and any corrections are recorded. |
| 8 | Linkage | Decisions, actions, and open items are carried into the decision log, RAID log, or requirements they affect. |

## Scoring anchors

Anchors describe the extreme scores; 1 and 2 interpolate between them.

| # | Score 0 (absent) | Score 3 (strong) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | No date, facilitator, or attendance, so authority cannot be judged. | The meeting, date, facilitator, note taker, and attendance are recorded, so the authority of what was decided can be judged. |
| 2 | No purpose or agenda, and missed items are not noted. | The purpose and the agenda items covered are stated, and items that were not reached are noted. |
| 3 | Discussion recorded as if it were a decision, or decisions missing. | Each decision is stated as a clear outcome with its rationale and owner, and discussion without a decision is not recorded as one. |
| 4 | Actions have no owner or date. | Each action is specific, has a single owner, and has a due date. |
| 5 | Open questions are lost rather than parked. | Unresolved questions are parked with an owner and a next step rather than lost. |
| 6 | The notes record the note taker's interpretation, not what was said. | Statements are attributed where attribution matters, and the notes record what was said, not the note taker's interpretation. |
| 7 | Notes were never circulated or confirmed. | The notes were circulated promptly and confirmed by participants, and any corrections are recorded. |
| 8 | Decisions and actions never reach the logs they affect. | Decisions, actions, and open items are carried into the decision log, RAID log, or requirements they affect. |

## Common failure modes

- A transcript instead of notes.
- Actions owned by "all".
- Notes circulated a week later, after memories have diverged.
- Decisions recorded in notes but never added to the decision log.

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or 20 or higher with a dimension at 0.
- Fail: below 14.

Dimensions 3 (decisions) and 4 (actions) are blocking: a score of 0 on either fails the document whatever the total. Record findings by severity with specific fixes, and route material issues back to the communication skill. A draft that does not pass may still be useful; its quality state must remain visible.
