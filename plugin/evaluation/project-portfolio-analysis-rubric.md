# Project Portfolio Analysis Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a project portfolio analysis supports sound investment decisions. The analysis maps the initiatives in a portfolio, their dependencies, and their priority. Used by the strategy and business-architecture skills. Based on BABOK Business Architecture Perspective (11.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Initiatives mapped | The initiatives in the portfolio are identified with their scope. |
| 2 | Value and cost | The value and cost of each initiative are assessed. |
| 3 | Dependencies | Dependencies between initiatives are identified. |
| 4 | Prioritisation | The initiatives are prioritised on a consistent basis. |
| 5 | Balance | The portfolio balance, across risk, horizon, and type, is assessed. |
| 6 | Strategy aligned | The portfolio aligns to the enterprise strategy. |
| 7 | Actionable | The analysis supports fund, defer, or stop decisions. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the strategy skill.
