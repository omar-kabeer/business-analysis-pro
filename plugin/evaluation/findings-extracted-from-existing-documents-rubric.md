# Findings Extracted from Existing Documents Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether document analysis findings are accurate and traceable. The findings are the requirements, rules, and facts extracted from existing documentation. Used by the document-analysis skill. Based on BABOK Document Analysis (10.18).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Source coverage | The relevant documents were reviewed, with the set covered stated. |
| 2 | Extraction accuracy | The findings accurately reflect the source, without distortion. |
| 3 | Traceable citations | Each finding cites the document and location it came from. |
| 4 | Relevance | The findings are relevant to the need or scope. |
| 5 | Currency | The currency and authority of each source are noted. |
| 6 | Conflicts | Conflicts between sources are surfaced rather than hidden. |
| 7 | Gaps | Information the documents do not answer is noted. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the document-analysis skill.
