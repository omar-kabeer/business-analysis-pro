# Prototype Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a prototype serves its purpose. A prototype is a partial or preliminary build, such as a mock-up, wireframe, or working model, used to explore or validate a solution. Used by the prototyping and ux skills. Based on BABOK Prototyping (10.36).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Purpose clear | The purpose of the prototype, whether to explore, validate, or demonstrate, is stated. |
| 2 | Type fit | The prototype type suits that purpose, whether throw-away or evolutionary, horizontal or vertical. |
| 3 | Key flows covered | The prototype covers the flows or questions it is meant to address. |
| 4 | Testable | It is concrete enough to test with users or stakeholders. |
| 5 | Requirements traceable | Its content traces to the requirements it represents. |
| 6 | Fidelity fit | The fidelity matches the purpose and stage. |
| 7 | Feedback captured | Feedback from using the prototype is captured. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the prototyping skill.
