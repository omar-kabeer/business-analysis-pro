# Item / Issue Log Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether an item or issue log tracks items to resolution. An item log records issues, actions, and open items with their owners and status. Used by the governance and ba-planning skills. Based on BABOK Item Tracking (10.26).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Items captured | Each item is described clearly. |
| 2 | Owned | Each item has an assigned owner. |
| 3 | Status current | The status of each item is current. |
| 4 | Priority | Each item has a priority or severity. |
| 5 | Dates | Raised and target resolution dates are recorded. |
| 6 | Resolution tracked | Resolution and closure are recorded. |
| 7 | Escalation | Overdue or blocked items are escalated per the process. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the governance skill.
