# Survey / Questionnaire and Results Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a survey is well-designed and its results sound. A survey or questionnaire gathers information from many respondents, and its results summarize what they said. Used by the elicitation and market-research skills. Based on BABOK Survey or Questionnaire (10.45).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Objective | The purpose of the survey and the questions it must answer are stated. |
| 2 | Unbiased questions | The questions are clear and free of leading or ambiguous wording. |
| 3 | Sample adequate | The sample is large and representative enough to draw conclusions. |
| 4 | Response rate | The response rate is reported and adequate. |
| 5 | Analysis | The results are analysed correctly, quantitatively and qualitatively. |
| 6 | Findings | The findings answer the objective and note their limitations. |
| 7 | Actionable | The results feed the next step of the work. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
