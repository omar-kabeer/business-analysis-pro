# Inspection Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a formal inspection was conducted soundly. An inspection is a formal review with defined roles that finds defects in a work product against entry and exit criteria. Used by the quality skill. Based on BABOK Reviews (10.37).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Formal roles | The inspection roles, such as moderator, reader, and recorder, are assigned. |
| 2 | Entry criteria | The entry criteria were met before the inspection began. |
| 3 | Coverage | The work product was inspected in full against the criteria. |
| 4 | Defects logged | Defects are logged and classified by severity. |
| 5 | Exit criteria | The exit criteria are defined and the outcome is judged against them. |
| 6 | Actions | Actions to resolve defects are assigned and owned. |
| 7 | Record | The inspection is recorded so the outcome is auditable. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the quality skill.
