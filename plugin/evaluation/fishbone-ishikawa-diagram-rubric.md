# Fishbone / Ishikawa Diagram Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a fishbone diagram reaches genuine root causes. A fishbone, or Ishikawa, diagram organizes the possible causes of a problem into categories. Used by the visual-modelling and quality skills. Based on BABOK Root Cause Analysis (10.40).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Problem stated | The effect or problem at the head of the diagram is stated clearly. |
| 2 | Categories | The cause categories are appropriate to the domain and cover the likely sources. |
| 3 | Causes not symptoms | The entries are causes, not restatements of the symptom. |
| 4 | Depth | Causes are explored to their roots, for example by asking why repeatedly. |
| 5 | Evidence | The causes are supported by evidence, not assertion. |
| 6 | Completeness | No major category of cause is left unexamined. |
| 7 | Actionable | The root causes identified can be acted on. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the quality skill.
