# Product Backlog Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a product backlog is well-ordered and ready. A product backlog is the prioritized list of work items for a product. Used by the product-owner and agile-coach skills. Based on BABOK Backlog Management (10.2).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Items well-formed | The items are INVEST-quality: independent, negotiable, valuable, estimable, small, and testable. |
| 2 | Ranked by value | The backlog is ordered by value and priority. |
| 3 | Refined | The top items are refined and ready for delivery. |
| 4 | Estimated | The near-term items are estimated. |
| 5 | Acceptance criteria | Ready items carry acceptance criteria. |
| 6 | Right size | Items near the top are small enough to deliver; larger items sit lower. |
| 7 | Owned and current | The backlog has a single owner and is kept current. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the product-owner skill.
