# Financial Analysis Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a financial analysis is sound. A financial analysis assesses the financial value of an investment using measures such as ROI, NPV, IRR, and payback. Used by the finance skill. Based on BABOK Financial Analysis (10.20).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Costs and benefits | Costs and benefits are identified and quantified. |
| 2 | Assumptions sourced | Every figure has a stated assumption and source. |
| 3 | Model consistent | The cash-flow model is internally consistent over a sensible horizon. |
| 4 | Metrics correct | ROI, NPV, IRR, and payback are computed correctly and interpreted honestly. |
| 5 | Sensitivity | A sensitivity or scenario analysis tests the key drivers. |
| 6 | Intangibles | Non-financial value is recorded alongside the numbers. |
| 7 | Conclusion | The conclusion follows honestly from the analysis. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the finance skill.
