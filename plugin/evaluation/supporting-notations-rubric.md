# Supporting Notations Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a supporting process notation is applied soundly. Supporting notations, such as BPMN, SIPOC, swimlane, or FMEA, augment process work with a specific representation. Used by the process-modelling skill. Based on BABOK Business Process Management Perspective (11.5). Apply the notation to its standard, do not reproduce the standard.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Right notation | The notation chosen fits the purpose of the analysis. |
| 2 | Valid use | The notation is used correctly, following its standard. |
| 3 | Complete | The representation is complete for what it models. |
| 4 | Consistent | Symbols and conventions are used consistently. |
| 5 | Purpose served | The notation serves the analysis, rather than being applied for its own sake. |
| 6 | Currency | The version of the notation used is current. |
| 7 | Readable | The result is legible and can be used unaided. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the process-modelling skill.
