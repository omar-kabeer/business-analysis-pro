# Process Performance Measures Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether process performance measures reflect how a process performs. Process performance measures quantify the performance of a business process against its goals. Used by the process-modelling and business-intelligence skills. Based on BABOK Business Process Management Perspective (11.5).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reflect the process | The measures reflect the performance of the process, such as time, cost, quality, and volume. |
| 2 | Aligned | The measures align to the process goals and to enterprise objectives. |
| 3 | Baseline and target | Each measure has a baseline and a target. |
| 4 | Collectable | The data can be collected practically. |
| 5 | Balanced | The measures balance efficiency and effectiveness, not one alone. |
| 6 | Valid | The measures are valid indicators, not proxies that mislead. |
| 7 | Reporting | How and when the measures are reported is defined. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the process-modelling skill.
