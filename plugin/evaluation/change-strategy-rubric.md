# Change Strategy Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a change strategy is ready to guide the move from the current state to the future state. The change strategy describes the nature of the change, the chosen approach among alternatives, and the transition states along the way. Used by the strategy and quality skills and applied to any strategy produced from `templates/change-strategy.md`. Based on BABOK Define Change Strategy (6.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Context of the change | The nature and context of the change are described. |
| 2 | Alternatives assessed | Alternative change strategies are identified and compared. |
| 3 | Justification | Why the chosen strategy is the best approach is argued from the comparison. |
| 4 | Investment and resources | The investment and resources required to reach the future state are stated. |
| 5 | Value realization | How the enterprise realizes value after the solution is delivered is described. |
| 6 | Transition states | The transition states along the way are defined, each showing what is complete and what value is realized. |
| 7 | Key stakeholders | The key stakeholders in the change are identified. |
| 8 | Scope alignment | The strategy is consistent with the solution scope and the future state description. |
| 9 | Risks | Risks to the change are identified with responses and owners. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 27):

- Pass: 22 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 16 to 21, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 16, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the strategy skill.
