# Workshop Outputs Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a workshop produced its intended outputs. Workshop outputs are the decisions, agreed deliverables, and models a facilitated workshop produces. Used by the elicitation skill. Based on BABOK Workshops (10.50).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Goal met | The workshop achieved the goal it was run for. |
| 2 | Decisions captured | The decisions made are captured with their rationale. |
| 3 | Deliverables agreed | The deliverables produced are agreed by the participants. |
| 4 | Participation | The right participants attended and contributed. |
| 5 | Actions | Actions and open items are recorded with owners. |
| 6 | Shared understanding | The outputs reflect a shared understanding. |
| 7 | Human-facilitated | The facilitation is acknowledged as human-led, with its judgment recorded. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
