# Walkthrough Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a walkthrough was conducted soundly. A walkthrough is an author-led review in which the author guides participants through a work product to gather feedback. Used by the quality skill. Based on BABOK Reviews (10.37).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Author-led | The author guides participants through the work product. |
| 2 | Participants | The participants are the right people to give useful feedback. |
| 3 | Coverage | The relevant parts of the work product are walked through. |
| 4 | Feedback captured | The feedback and issues raised are captured. |
| 5 | Actions | Actions arising are assigned and owned. |
| 6 | Purpose met | The purpose of the walkthrough, whether understanding or defect-finding, is met. |
| 7 | Record | The walkthrough and its outcome are recorded. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the quality skill.
