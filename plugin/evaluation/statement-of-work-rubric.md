# Statement of Work (SOW) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a statement of work defines the work and its acceptance clearly. An SOW describes the work and deliverables to be performed under a contract. Used by the procurement-contracts and ba-planning skills. Based on BABOK procurement and agreement practice.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Deliverables | The deliverables to be produced are defined. |
| 2 | Scope of work | The work to be performed, and what is out of scope, is stated. |
| 3 | Timeline | The schedule, milestones, and dependencies are stated. |
| 4 | Acceptance criteria | The criteria for accepting each deliverable are defined. |
| 5 | Responsibilities | The responsibilities of each party are clear. |
| 6 | Commercials | Price, payment terms, and any assumptions are stated. |
| 7 | Governance | How changes and issues are handled is defined. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the procurement-contracts skill.
