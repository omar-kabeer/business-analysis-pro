# Stakeholder Analysis Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a stakeholder analysis is complete and correct enough to drive engagement. Stakeholder analysis identifies the stakeholders, assesses their influence and interest, and assigns responsibility. Used by the elicitation and quality skills. Based on BABOK Stakeholder List, Map, or Personas (10.43).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Coverage | All stakeholders and groups are identified, with none missed. |
| 2 | Influence and interest | Influence and interest, or impact, are assessed for each stakeholder. |
| 3 | Classification | Stakeholders are placed correctly, for example in the onion diagram rings or the influence quadrants. |
| 4 | RACI | Where a RACI matrix is used, every activity has a Responsible and an Accountable, with no gaps. |
| 5 | Attitude | The attitude or level of support of each stakeholder is captured. |
| 6 | Engagement implication | The analysis drives an engagement approach for each stakeholder. |
| 7 | Evidence-based | The assessments are evidenced, not guessed. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
