# Story Map Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a story map organizes work around the user journey soundly. A story map arranges stories along the user's journey and into release slices. Used by the product-owner and ux skills. Based on BABOK Agile Perspective (11.1).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Backbone | The user activities form a coherent backbone across the top. |
| 2 | End to end | The map covers the journey end to end. |
| 3 | Steps sequenced | The steps under each activity are in a sensible order. |
| 4 | Stories placed | Stories are placed under the right step. |
| 5 | Release slices | Horizontal slices define coherent releases or increments. |
| 6 | Value first | The first slice delivers a usable, valuable outcome. |
| 7 | Gaps visible | Gaps and missing steps are visible on the map. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the product-owner skill.
