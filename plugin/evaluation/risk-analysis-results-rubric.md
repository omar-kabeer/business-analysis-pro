# Risk Analysis Results Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether risk analysis results are complete and actionable. Risk analysis results capture the understanding of risks to achieving the future state and the strategies to deal with them. Used by the risk-analysis and quality skills and applied to risk analysis results produced during strategy analysis. Based on BABOK Assess Risks (6.3).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Risks well-formed | Each risk states the condition and its effect on the value of the change. |
| 2 | Likelihood and impact | Each risk is assessed for likelihood and impact. |
| 3 | Prioritised | Risks are ranked by exposure. |
| 4 | Owned | Each risk has an owner. |
| 5 | Responses | Each risk has a response: avoid, transfer, mitigate, accept, or increase. |
| 6 | Uncertainty acknowledged | Unknowns and the uncertainty in the assessment are acknowledged. |
| 7 | Evidence | The assessment draws on evidence, expert judgment, or history, not guesswork. |
| 8 | Traceable | The risks tie to the business objectives, change strategy, and future state. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 24):

- Pass: 20 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 14 to 19, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 14, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the risk-analysis skill.
