# Recommended Actions Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether recommended actions to increase solution value are sound and defensible. The recommendation is the course of action, such as adjust, replace, retire, or invest, that will increase the value the solution delivers. Used by the solution-evaluation and quality skills and applied to recommended actions produced during solution evaluation. Based on BABOK Recommend Actions to Increase Solution Value (8.5).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Options assessed | Adjust, replace, retire, and invest options are considered. |
| 2 | Value basis | The recommendation is grounded in potential value and the solution and enterprise limitations. |
| 3 | Justification | Why the recommended action is the best is argued from the analysis. |
| 4 | Cost and benefit | Future investment is weighed against future benefit, with sunk cost disregarded. |
| 5 | Risk | The risks of the recommended action are identified. |
| 6 | Actionable and owned | The next steps are specific, owned, and feasible. |
| 7 | Traceable | The recommendation ties to the business objectives and the performance analysis. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the solution-evaluation skill.
