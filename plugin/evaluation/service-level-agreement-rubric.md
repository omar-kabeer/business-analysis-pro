# Service Level Agreement (SLA) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a service level agreement sets measurable, enforceable service terms. An SLA states the agreed measures of success and service constraints between a provider and a customer. Used by the procurement-contracts and quality skills and applied to any SLA produced from `templates/service-level-agreement.md`. Based on BABOK procurement and agreement practice.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Service defined | The services covered by the agreement are described. |
| 2 | Measurable levels | The service levels are stated as measurable targets. |
| 3 | Measurement method | How each level is measured and reported is defined. |
| 4 | Remedies | The remedies or penalties for missing a level are stated. |
| 5 | Responsibilities | The responsibilities of provider and customer are clear. |
| 6 | Review cadence | The cadence for reviewing the agreement is defined. |
| 7 | Term and change | The term, and how the agreement is changed, are stated. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the procurement-contracts skill.
