# Request for Quote (RFQ) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a request for quote will return comparable prices. An RFQ solicits price and solution information for a defined set of goods or services. Used by the procurement-contracts and vendor-evaluation skills. Based on BABOK procurement and solicitation practice.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Specifications clear | The goods or services required are specified precisely. |
| 2 | Quantities | The quantities and units are stated. |
| 3 | Price format | The required price breakdown and format are defined so quotes can be compared. |
| 4 | Delivery terms | Delivery, timing, and service terms are stated. |
| 5 | Validity | The period for which the quote must remain valid is stated. |
| 6 | Process and timeline | The submission process and deadline are clear. |
| 7 | Terms | Payment and contractual terms are included. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the procurement-contracts skill.
