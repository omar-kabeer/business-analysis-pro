# Focus Group Findings Report Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a focus group findings report is representative and evidenced. The report summarizes the themes and findings from a focus group session. Used by the elicitation and market-research skills. Based on BABOK Focus Groups (10.21).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Representative | The participants represented the target group, and this is stated. |
| 2 | Themes evidenced | The themes are supported by what participants actually said. |
| 3 | Balanced | Majority and minority views are both reported. |
| 4 | Bias controlled | The report accounts for facilitation and group bias. |
| 5 | Objective | The findings are reported without slant to a preferred conclusion. |
| 6 | Context | The session context and method are recorded. |
| 7 | Actionable | The findings feed the next step of the work. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the elicitation skill.
