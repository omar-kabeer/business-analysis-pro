# Business Model Canvas Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a business model canvas is complete and internally consistent. The canvas describes a business model across nine building blocks. Used by the strategy and product-manager skills. Based on BABOK Business Model Canvas (10.8).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Nine blocks | All nine blocks are populated: value propositions, customer segments, channels, relationships, revenue streams, key resources, activities, partnerships, and cost structure. |
| 2 | Value proposition | The value proposition is clear and specific. |
| 3 | Segments | The customer segments are named and distinct. |
| 4 | Fit | The value proposition fits the segments it serves. |
| 5 | Economics | Revenue streams and cost structure are consistent with the rest of the model. |
| 6 | Internal consistency | The blocks reinforce rather than contradict each other. |
| 7 | Evidence | The model reflects evidence or stated assumptions, not pure guesswork. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the strategy skill.
