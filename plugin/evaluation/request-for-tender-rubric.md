# Request for Tender (RFT) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a request for tender is a fair, complete invitation to bid. An RFT is a formal, structured invitation to suppliers to bid for a contract. Used by the procurement-contracts and vendor-evaluation skills. Based on BABOK procurement and solicitation practice.

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Formal terms | The formal terms and conditions of the tender are stated. |
| 2 | Requirements | The requirements and specifications to be met are complete. |
| 3 | Evaluation criteria | The criteria and weighting for evaluating bids are stated. |
| 4 | Submission rules | The rules for a valid submission, including format and deadline, are defined. |
| 5 | Fairness | The process gives all bidders equal information and opportunity. |
| 6 | Compliance | Legal, regulatory, and procurement obligations are addressed. |
| 7 | Contact and process | The point of contact and the tender process are clear. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the procurement-contracts skill.
