# Requirements Change Assessment Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a requirements change assessment is thorough enough to decide on a proposed change. The assessment is the recommendation to approve, modify, or deny a proposed change to requirements. Used by the change-control and quality skills and applied to any assessment produced from `templates/requirements-change-assessment.md`. Based on BABOK Assess Requirements Changes (5.4).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Impact analysed | The effect of the change on requirements, coverage, and allocation is assessed. |
| 2 | Cost, benefit, risk | The cost, benefit, and risk of the change are weighed. |
| 3 | Clear recommendation | The recommendation to approve, modify, or deny is stated plainly. |
| 4 | Justification | The recommendation follows from the analysis. |
| 5 | Stakeholders consulted | The affected stakeholders, including the sponsor and tester, are consulted. |
| 6 | Traceable | The change is traced to the requirements it touches. |
| 7 | Governance fit | The assessment runs through the defined change-control process. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the change-control skill.
