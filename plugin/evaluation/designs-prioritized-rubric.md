# Designs (prioritized) Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether prioritized designs are ranked soundly enough to steer delivery. Prioritization ranks design options and components by their relative importance and the sequence in which they will be built. Used by the architecture and governance skills. Based on BABOK Prioritize Requirements (5.3).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Basis explicit | The basis for priority, whether value, cost, risk, or sequence, is stated. |
| 2 | Method | A prioritization method is applied consistently across the designs. |
| 3 | Interdependencies | Dependencies between design components are identified and reflected in priority. |
| 4 | Value maximised | The prioritization aims at maximum value for the stakeholders. |
| 5 | Stakeholder agreement | The priorities are agreed with the stakeholders. |
| 6 | Ongoing | The priorities are revisited as the context changes. |
| 7 | Traceable | The priority is recorded against each design element. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the architecture skill.
