# Review Record / Defect Log Evaluation Rubric

A repeatable rubric for judging whether a review record captures the outcome of a review soundly. The record documents what was reviewed, the defects found, and the outcome against exit criteria. Used by the quality skill. Based on BABOK Reviews (10.37).

## Scoring scale

For each dimension score 0 to 3: 0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong.

## Dimensions

| # | Dimension | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Scope recorded | What was reviewed, by whom, and when is recorded. |
| 2 | Coverage | The record shows the work product was reviewed in full. |
| 3 | Defects logged | Defects are logged with enough detail to act on. |
| 4 | Classified | Each defect is classified by type and severity. |
| 5 | Actions | Actions to resolve defects are assigned and owned. |
| 6 | Exit criteria | The outcome is judged against defined exit criteria. |
| 7 | Auditable | The record is complete enough to be auditable. |

## Result

Total the scores (maximum 21):

- Pass: 17 or higher with no dimension at 0.
- Pass with changes: 12 to 16, or a single quick-to-fix dimension at 1.
- Fail: below 12, or any dimension at 0.

Record findings by severity with a specific fix, and route material issues back to the quality skill.
